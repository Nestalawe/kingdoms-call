## How each kit played (and the round-24 changes)

**Undead.** Skeletal Armies alone builds a host, slowly and only at a barrow: 40% and 2 companies a cast at Necromancy 1, 80% and 3 a cast at Necromancy 5–7 (practice plus the barrow's +2). By turn 11 the realm had 25 skeletons under three necromancers, but Average skeletons cannot fight: the king's 6 were crushed by 5 Druids holding a plain (turn 8), and hero-led hosts of 4–12 lost to a 2-company neutral garrison four times. Bind the Bones to Crack (round 24) turned it: 14 companies, 6 Crack, won every battle after turn 12 losing one company in five. The cap at Crack never bit in 16 turns; the price did: the realm sat at 0–8g from turn 11 and twice lost a living company to unpaid wages. Blight the Land once (tax 3 → 1, +4g, a free garrison). Desecrate, Lichdom, The soul is taken: never reached.

**Evil.** Terror −30% took five neutral provinces by turn 7; Tribute of Fear paid 2–7g a turn; Garrok raised 10 skeletons from battlefields; the war-without-warning walk into the empty Divine capital (turn 9) worked and was reversed a phase later. The altar promotion never fired (the capital is hallowed, not consecrated), so the round-24 change was not tested; no monster province was in reach, so neither were Dark Pact and Savage Fury. Then Evil met Good 8's king and lost five battles of six (4 scattered at Marldelve, 6 of 10 attacking Stonevines, 5 of 6 at Grimcrag, the capital on the last turn). Peace offers went unanswered. A one-host kit has no answer to a Tactical 8 defender.

**Divine.** Consecrate ×4 (Tithes +7–8g a turn on a 24-tax realm). Divine Power from consecrations (round 24) worked: 4 consecrated = 2 points, +1 on Crusade = 3, so Revelation came on turn 11; Powers 4–6 stayed out of reach and Sanctuary and Grace never mattered. Holy defence and the Proclaimed Crusade ×1.5 won both defences: Evil's walk-in reversed at once, Pagan 7's five Elite repelled with no loss after a storm had destroyed 2 and demoted 4 of the Divine 8. The Last Rites left no graves; the sword consecrated Hornsands as it fell. The first Crusade was wasted: proclaimed the turn Marldelve fell to Good 8, the column was "turned back at the border, at peace with Realm8". Divine stayed at 5–6 provinces: repelled three times early, then boxed in by two realms it was at peace with.

**Good.** Both Good realms thrived. The Villages on tax ≤5 (round 24) paid +3 on four or five provinces each. Light the Beacons (round 24: wage-free, 3 turns, then home) was lit three times, 5 Veterans for 10g, no wage bill, and went home on schedule; never attacked, so untested in defence, but no longer a trap. The Shield held Fangstone against nothing (Druidic 3 went round it). Call to Arms gave a Green garrison that fell at once. Good 8's king went Tactical 1 → 9 from battles (Knights errant) and won five of six against Evil; Good 2's king reached Tactical 9 and Druid 6 the same way. Good Works: nothing to clear. Reclaim: never tried.

**Druidic.** Druidic 3 took 15 provinces, six of them creature provinces (Parley ×1.5, a free beast each), grew Talonscar into jungle for 8g, and ended with 19 companies and wages 78 on tax 71: the richest land, the poorest treasury. Druidic 9 crushed the Undead's first attack with Crack bowmen on a plain, then lost everything to the bound host and to the League, which bought Morflats and Stonehold from under its armyless king. Breeding, Call of the Wild and healers' fees were marginal.

**Neutral.** Eight Trade Pacts: five unclaimed provinces (11–25g, defenders becoming the garrison) and three other realms' (Morflats 16g and Stonehold 32g from Druidic 9, Silverfen from the Undead, each with 0 defenders). The round-24 refusals held (no pact where a hero stood, none on a realm at war with the League), but the League could still buy Silverfen the turn after the Undead captured it, since the League was at peace with the Undead. Embargo twice on the Undead. Bands at 1–2g, Markets, a Sage 9. Wages 68 on tax 121, 429g unspent. Ransom: no duel offered itself.

**Pagan.** Favour ×1.5 by turn 9 (Pagan 0 five sites, Pagan 7 four) on every battle line. Call down the storm at 8g (round 24) was used three times: once on a neutral garrison, twice on Gilmoor against the Divine king (2 destroyed + 4 demoted, then 1 + 1): still worth 8g, no longer a province-winner, and the assault behind it was repelled. Blood Harvest made Pagan 7's whole host Elite by turn 12 on neutral kills. The Old Stones were never attacked; Wild Hunt, Blood Offering and the Ley not needed. Both Pagans ran deficits in turns 1–5, then became the richest realms on the map.

## Report and order problems (new)

1. A Proclaimed Crusade and a column of march are refused at a province a third realm took the same turn, with no refund: "turned back at the border — at peace". The Crusade's 6g and 3 turns were spent on nothing.
2. The Trade Pact refusal names every condition at once and never the one that applied (four refusals this game: a creature province, a hero present, and twice it was unclear).
3. A king who buys a province where his own hero does not stand ("here or next door") is fine, but Trade Pact bought Silverfen from a realm at peace that had captured it the turn before; a one-turn "no pact on land taken this turn" would stop the League following a conqueror.
4. Recruit orders at a province of another race print nothing when the type does not exist there; the player cannot tell which types a province recruits until he holds it.
5. The Undead's unpaid-wages line says "the skeletal dead ask for nothing" but skeletons are charged 1g each in the bill above it.
6. A defending king ordered away in the phase he is attacked stays put with no line saying why (King8 at Stonevines).
7. A king alone cannot be caught by a hero; King9 walked through three Undead-held provinces and the Undead king never had a phase to run him down (Encounter needs the king to stop fighting).
8. Three Melee 1 heroes died or fled to Weak lairs (an Imp Swarm killed Ozruk); the lair's "Weak" label should say the skill it expects.

## Three balance observations

1. **The Undead is now a slow burner that needs its king present for everything.** Without Harvest it took 11 turns to field a host that could beat a player, and only the king's Bind the Bones made it fight; every hero-led skeletal host lost. That is a fair place for it, but the wage bill (1g a skeleton, 10g of heroes, no land income) kept the realm at 0–8g from turn 11 even while winning. Suggest: skeletal companies cost 1g per two (or the Host-Leader discount counts skeletons double), so the Undead can afford to bind.
2. **A king with Tactical 8–9 is the strongest piece on the board, and Good gets there fastest.** Both Good kings reached Tactical 9 by turn 16 from battles alone (Knights errant doubling the gain); King8 won five of six against Evil's whole kit, King2 never lost. Suggest Knights errant doubles only Melee, or Tactical gains stop doubling at 5.
3. **Peace walls decide the end-game more than kits.** Divine, Druidic 3 and Good 2 were boxed in by neighbours they were at peace with and could only grow by declaring war, while Neutral grew by buying from everyone. Either let a hero cross a friendly border alone (so Divine could reach Eastmeadow), or give every realm one free border crossing a game, or make the Trade Pact wait a turn on freshly taken land.
