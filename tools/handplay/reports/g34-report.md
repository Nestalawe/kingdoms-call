## Report and order problems (new)

1. **A ransom is paid at the end of the turn it is set, and the freed hero stands in the captor's province.** The same hero was taken three times in four turns.
2. **The pact quote uses the awakened tax** (40g for a tax-2 desert), and a notice is free: the League can pin a garrison for the price of one order.
3. **The Reckoning refusal quotes a share (19%) below the land-tax share (21.8%) and a bar of 25%, while the log had said "the crown's price falls to 30%".** Two shares and two bars: a player cannot tell when a claim will stand.
4. **The bare-capital warning named a host of a realm at peace** (Druid capital, Good8 hero next door, turn 20).
5. **Blessed levies of another race carry −20%** under the Divine king, and nothing on the recruit order says so.
6. **Defend rode six hosts out to defeats** (Divine king, Neutral king, two Pagan heroes, a Druid hero, an Undead hero with a blighted garrison).
7. **A storm can be called from stones in a province the realm has lost.**
8. **A beaten host that is "not routed" is not captured**, and nothing tells the captor why his ransom plan did nothing (turn 15, a one-company host).

## Three balance observations

1. **Ransom should cost the loser something.** Suggest: the captive is held at least one full turn, the price is 3g a skill level (or a tenth of the loser's treasury if more), and he is freed at his own capital.
2. **The Undead still cannot hold what they take, and the player has to remember to.** Suggest: a barrow the realm holds keeps one grave's worth of its dead as a standing garrison on its own (the "dead of this soil" rule extended from blighted land to barrows).
3. **Druidic left alone is the strongest opening (10 provinces on turn 5), and only wages stop it.** Suggest "the pack submits" only when a hero of Druid 2 or more leads the attack, and no free garrison beast in a province taken that turn.
