The new report line ("Divine Power rises to 4 … Pilgrim roads") came every time it changed: good. Still overshoots: all six gifts by turn 12 and Power 13 at the end, three consecrations a point is not enough once the sword consecrates. Sanctuary healing never showed a line (no hero wounded on holy ground). And Divine finished on 917g: it could not spend it. Recruiting in conquered provinces failed outright when the province's race had none of the asked-for units, and Uriel's 12-company host fought at "Druid shortfall −20%" on every unit (the command limit).

**Good (R1 second on land, R9 fourth).** Villages +3/+2 paid on most Good land from turn 3; R1 ended with 838g. **Knights errant to Tactical 5:** R1's king still reached Tactical 8 by turn 15, Bryony and Vervain Tactical 6, from battles alone: the doubling stopped at 5 but plain experience carried on. **Beacons:** lit three times (12g, 12g, 6g); each muster served wage-free and went home with a line; once (turn 15) Evil R8 took a province the turn its muster left. **Pagan (third).** Five stones woken by turn 14 (Favour ×1.4), Blood Harvest money, and a second host from the pressed levy. **Storm at 8g:** cast once, and it destroyed the Neutral king's last two companies so the Pagan king walked in. Without Harvest there was no way to rebuild skeletons from those fields.

## Report and order problems (new)

1. **Defender swing**, again and larger: 365 v 169 (6 of 13 attackers lost, 0 defenders), 274 v 170 (11 of 19 lost, 0), 19 Druidic attacking 10 Evil (11 lost, 0), Divine 15 v Evil 12 (7 lost, 1). A 1.6× attacker routinely loses most of its host for nothing.
2. **A king who is repelled retreats, and his later move orders run from the new place** (my Undead king's "move NW" went into the capital instead of the province beside it). The order form should warn that later moves start from wherever the battle left him.
3. **Recruiting in a conquered province of another race fails silently per type** ("not raised here" for every type asked); Divine sat on 917g largely because of it. The province's recruit list should be visible before ordering.
4. **"Druid shortfall −20%"** is printed on all twelve humanoid companies of an over-large Divine host: it is the command limit, mislabelled.
5. **Ransom has no path that works against a moving hero**: Encounter "keeps the trail but cannot close" and nothing tells the player the odds.
6. **Raising skeletons in an empty enemy province captures it** (Ghouric took Gromgrove that way). Probably right, but nothing says so.
7. **Grand Design** (Sage 9) announced as a new order with no order name the player can type.
8. A peace overture still reads "went unanswered this year" the turn it is sent; mutual 'treat' now makes peace (not alliance) one turn later, which is clear.

## Three balance observations

1. **The defender's swing decides wars more than any kit.** Every war in this game ended when an attacker with 1.5× the strength lost most of its host and the defender lost nothing. Suggest a cap on how much of the loser's host can be destroyed in one battle when the strength ratio is under 2:1 (say half), or let the attacker's losses scale with the margin.
2. **Divine Power still runs away under Crusade.** Three consecrations a point plus "the sword consecrates" plus a point for the Crusade gave Power 13. Suggest consecrations count to +3 at most, or provinces consecrated by the sword do not count toward Power.
3. **Big treasuries have nothing to buy.** Divine 917g, Good 838g, Pagan 317g at the end; the pressed levy helped (second hosts in five realms) but recruiting is still gated by what one province offers, one company of a kind per order. Suggest a "muster" order at a consecrated or home province that raises any of the realm's own race's units, and a hire-a-wanderer market for gold.
