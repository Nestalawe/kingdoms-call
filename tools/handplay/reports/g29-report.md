## Each alignment, and how round 26 played

**Undead (eighth).** With no barrow anywhere near its capital, the realm lived on battlefield graves. Its two heroes cross-trained Necromancy to 3 and 4 by turn 3 and raised 10 skeletons over the game; 9 casts fizzled, most of them the king's. *Skeleton garrisons on blighted land work:* Lisandra blighted Westhold on turn 6 and set two skeletons there as a garrison. They stayed to the end with no necromancer, beside the free skeleton the blighted soil raised. They held nothing that mattered, because nobody attacked Westhold. *The 1g wage* made no difference that I could see: the Undead still ended with 264g unspent. Bind the Bones to Crack was used twice, and the cap never mattered. The Undead lost the war it started with Pagan R0 (Thrain's two Elite forest companies held Gromroot) and lost four provinces to Good R5. In each case Good walked into land the Undead had left empty while its hosts were elsewhere.

**Evil (R3 won; R7 eliminated).** *The Dark Pact from afar* worked on turn 7: 15g and 8 HP for a Manticore, before the realm held any monster province. After that, every other turn brought a normal-price pact (7.5g, then 6g once Basilisks were bred). *Every other turn* slowed the stack, but "the pits yield" chained a free monster on two captures, so R3 still ended with five Manticores and two Basilisks. The pacts' blood cost left King3 at 7 of 27 HP. *Half wages only at home:* the monsters marched with the king, so they paid full wages, and R3 still ended with 327g. The altar promotion never came up, because no Evil host reached consecrated land. R7 shows the other side of the kit: no monster province, 0g by turn 7, three companies disbanded in one turn, and its capital lost to a walk-in on turn 8.

**Divine (sixth).** *Power capped, with holy sites counted once:* Power ran 1, 3, 2, 4, 3, 5 and 4. It no longer runs away, and it peaked only under Proclaimed Crusades. The report line came all ten times Power changed. Sanctuary healed the king twice. The Crusade's "sword consecrates" twice turned a retaken Goldcrag into holy ground at once. Divine's war on Pagan R9 was a stalemate: six assaults on the Pagan capital Runevale all failed, and Goldcrag changed hands four times under seven Pagan storms. The realm ended with 477g unspent, because its heroes could not recruit in the provinces where they stood.

**Pagan (R0 fifth, R9 seventh).** *The storm capped at two destroyed* was cast 15 times. It destroyed 0, 1 or 2 companies a cast (13 in all), and hit the cap four times. It still demoted freely, 10 companies in one cast on Good's Urweald host. It softened hosts but never took a province alone, and 8g against hoards of 170 to 400g was never a real cost. The Old Stones ×1.6 held Fangbriar against 10 Good companies on turn 15. R0 still lost three provinces to Good walk-ins while its king and host were far away, then made peace with Good (turn 17) and the Undead (turn 18). R9 took the Evil R7 capital on turn 8 and finished R7 by turn 10. It then lost five eastern provinces to Evil's surprise war at the end.

**Good (R8 third, R5 fourth, both rich).** The Beacons were lit five times, and each muster went home on schedule with a line. They decided three defences: Good R8's capital and Startangle on turn 13 against Pagan R0, and Dreadcrag and Savagedune against the Undead king on turns 15 and 17, with the Shield ×1.75. *Knights errant:* both kings stopped at Tactical 6, and Verael reached 6. The Villages and walk-ins into empty enemy land left both Good realms with the biggest hoards: 828g and 640g.

**Neutral (eliminated turn 15).** The king died in a duel on turn 3, and the heir needed a skill allocation. *The pact's turn of notice* worked as written. On turn 7 the envoys opened talks on Evil's Dunsmoor, both realms were told, and the price was quoted at 32g (twice the price). The seal on turn 9 was refused with the right reason: "another realm's hero stands in Dunsmoor". The League never had 32g in hand when it was free to seal. After a second notice on turn 10, Evil declared war and took Bramroot, then the capital and the rest. *Ransom in a pre-battle duel was never reached.* Valka carried the challenge-and-ransom plan, but when Evil attacked the capital, "Aldreth the Scarred takes command of the combined host": the king commanded, and kings are not taken for ransom.

**Druidic (second on land).** It never went to war. Grow the Wild turned the desert capital to jungle on turn 3, "the pack submits" gave a Mammoth, a Forest Ent and Stone Giants, and the realm reached 18 provinces. Perrin was repelled three times at Blightfell by one guarding hero. The realm ended with 324g unspent.

**The bare-capital warning** fired six times: four for the Undead, once for Pagan R0 and once for Pagan R9. It was accurate each time. Twice, Good then walked into Undead land that was not the capital. Once the Undead capital was empty because the king, on Defend, had ridden out to hold a neighbour, and the warning was the only sign of it.

## Report and order problems (new)

1. **When a League hero with the ransom plan defends beside his king, the king takes command** ("takes command of the combined host"), so the ransom path in a pre-battle duel cannot open. A defending League hero should keep his duel and his plan, or the plan should pass to the commander.
2. **A Dark Pact's blood cost has no floor except 1 HP.** The Evil king went to 7 of 27 HP after four pacts, with nothing to warn the player the next duel could kill him.
3. **A storm on a province with nothing to hit** costs 8g and reports "0 destroyed in all, 0 fallen a tier" (turns 14 and 18). The order could refuse an empty target, or the line could say there was nothing there.
4. **The pact notice quotes the price "at today's garrison"**, but the League is not told the price if the garrison changes. Nothing reminds the League that the notice lapses after two turns.
5. **Recruiting is still refused, type by type, in provinces of another race** (Pagan at Anvilwold and Fangbriar, Divine at Marlmarch), with no list shown first. This is why Divine and Pagan R9 ended with 400 to 480g.
6. **A hero of a realm at peace may lie in wait inside another realm's capital for ten turns** (Evil's Volkar in the Neutral capital), spying every turn, and nothing asks him to leave.
7. **Mutual 'treat' made an immediate peace this time** ("are now NEUTRAL"), and it was clear. A one-sided treat still reads "went unanswered this year" the turn it is sent.

## Three balance observations

1. **Surprise war plus a monster stack is the winning pattern.** Evil R3 took 11 provinces in its last 8 turns, nine of them walked into or taken in the turn war was declared. Leaving the surprise war as it is, the brake could go on the stack. "The pits yield" a chained monster could happen at most once per province, and a Dark Pact could need the king above half his HP. Either would keep Evil's best army without letting it double every few turns.
2. **The storm is now fair on destruction but not on demotion.** The two-company cap held, but one cast demoted 10 Good companies, and seven casts kept Divine from ever holding Goldcrag. Cap the demotions too (say four a cast), or let a province be stormed only once every two turns.
3. **An Undead realm with no barrow nearby has no engine.** This one never got more than about 12 skeletons and finished eighth. Its best moment was a 1g skeleton garrison on blighted land that nobody tested. Place a barrow within two hexes of every Undead capital (as in earlier rounds' suggestions), so the realm's opening does not depend on the map.
