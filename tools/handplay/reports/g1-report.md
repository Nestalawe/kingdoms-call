# g1 — Divine (Human) v Evil (Elven) v Pagan (Dwarven), seed 101, 16 turns, hand-played as every realm

## Result
Evil won on every measure: 14 provinces, 244g in hand, 82g tax a turn, a 14-company host entirely Elite/Crack that never lost a company to a player. Divine finished second (8 provinces, 100g, 52g tax) after peaking at 10 provinces and 90g tax on turn 7, then losing five consecrated provinces to Evil's walk-in. Pagan finished last (4 provinces, 34g), bled by wages from turn 4 and beaten in every open-field attack it made, though it won every defence of its own stones and destroyed 16 Divine companies with the storm.

Course of the game: turns 1–7 everyone expands into neutral land (Divine fastest, with Crusade ×1.5 against every monster-held province and Tithes from consecrating as it went; Evil second, Terror −30% making every neutral battle a formality; Pagan slowest, waking stones but over-hiring heroes). Turn 7 all three meet Divine. Turn 8 Evil declares war and strikes the same turn; Divine and Pagan declare and lose a turn. Turns 8–13 Evil takes Divine's empty consecrated provinces one a turn (Wolddown, Goldvines, Brokencopse, Feralfield, Leafroot, Bloodwells), promoting its whole host at each altar it casts down; Pagan storms Silverhollow six times; Divine's king kills three heroes in duels but loses both pitched battles it attacks. Turn 13 Divine takes Runehollow from Pagan; turn 16 Eldervines.

## Divine (Realm0)
- Used and mattered: Consecrate (7 provinces by turn 8; tax 90 with +22 from Tithes), Crusade ×1.5 against the wild (every early conquest), Holy defence ×1.5 (Bloodwells held twice against Torga), Blessed levies (Orc Garrisons and Black Arrows mustered as Veterans at Bloodwells), Proclaim Crusade (twice: on Pagan turns 9–11, on Evil turns 15–17; the 3-turn wait was honoured), Hounds of God + Holy wrath (the king, alone, slew Freya, Vorn and Sombra; three duels, three kills), Last Rites line shown at Bloodwells.
- Used, didn't matter: Pilgrim Roads / Divine Power (Divine held no holy site until turn 13, so Power stayed 0 all game: no Sanctuary, Grace abounding, Revelation or Pilgrim Roads; consecrating 7 provinces gave no Power). Spy (Mosswen, Spy 3: three tries, "yields little" twice, and the order only works where the hero stands). Divine Grace: no Divine hero died, so untested.
- Unused: Church Triumphant, Absolution (never reached). Encounter against a king inside a host is impossible ("turned away by the sentries").
- Strong: the economy (Tithes gave Divine the biggest treasury from turn 5 to turn 10) and the king as a duellist under Crusade. Weak: Divine has no way to hold what it consecrates: an empty consecrated province is a free promotion for Evil (see below), and Holy defence needs a garrison the Divine player has to pay for. Fun: the duel chases and the Crusade proclamation; frustrating: watching Evil walk through five empty provinces while the king was two hexes away with 6 companies.

## Evil (Realm1)
- Used and mattered: Terror (−30% on every neutral province next to a victory; Terror also shown on sea hexes, which looks wrong), Spread Dread (useful on turn 1–2 to seed Terror on unclaimed neighbours before the first fight; idle afterwards, since victories lay 2 stacks and the order never raises above 1), Dominion of the Pits ×1.5 (Savagescree, Basilisks chained), Savage Fury ×1.5/×1.25 (every battle after a monster joined), Reign of Fear ×1.5 (monsters under own Terror), Dark Pact (twice: 6g and 40% HP each; refused once at the beast cap), the pits breed (3 Basilisks in Savagescree's garrison by turn 16), Beast-masters (4 beasts with Druid 2), Evil looting (+1–4g on every capture), Tribute of Fear (+2–4g a turn), "casts down the altars" (every company of the host gained a level each time it took a consecrated province: 5 provinces, about 30 promotions, the whole host Elite by turn 13), the betrayal declaration (war takes effect at once; everyone else waits a turn).
- Unused: nothing in the kit went unused; Cadwal's Spy 1→4 revealed defenders once per province where he stood.
- Strong: everything stacks. 457 v 106 at Bloodwells was Savage Fury ×1.5 × Reign of Fear ×1.5 on a host promoted five times for free. Fun: as Evil, every turn was a win; as the other two, Evil was not beatable after turn 9. Weak: a small second host (Bramwen's 3–4 Lances) lost three times to a 3-company neutral garrison; Evil's strength is one doom-stack, and its conquests are as empty as Divine's (Seraphine seized two back with one Green company).

## Pagan (Realm2)
- Used and mattered: Rite of Awakening (3 sites: +3 tax each and Favour to ×1.48), Favour of the Old Gods (×1.16 → ×1.48 shown in every battle), The Old Stones ×1.6 (held Runehollow and Eldervines one round each; the king's single Elite company held Runehollow against 5), Blood Offering (Free Lances → Crossbows two tiers to Elite), Rite of the Wild Hunt (twice: 2 Veteran Ents for 4g, wage-free, 2 turns; the first time the king couldn't command them, −40%), Blood Harvest (+2g a kill, ~24g in all, and promotions: Crack→Elite twice), Call down the storm (7 times on Divine provinces, 28g: 16 companies destroyed and 15 demoted; the best gold-to-damage ratio of any order in the game), Spiritwrack/Choking Spores lines from the Ents.
- Unused: Rite of the Ley (never had two woken sites a host needed to jump between), The ancestors watch (Freya and Vorn died, the sight was not visibly reported to me).
- Strong: defence of woken stones and the storm. Weak: the economy (five heroes by turn 4 put wages above tax for eight turns; Pagan never had more than 39g), and attacking: four attacks on provinces held by players or neutrals were all repelled (Bloodwells ×2, Silverhollow, and Torga's first Bloodwells try), with the Ent handing Divine its Crusade ×1.5 against creatures. Fun: the storm and the rites; frustrating: no gold to use them with, and the mercenary bands the beaten defenders left were never affordable.

## Report and order-format problems found
1. War declared by Divine/Pagan takes effect at the end of the turn, so the same-turn march is "turned back at the border" and Proclaim Crusade "none answers". Nothing in the orders tells a player this; Evil's declaration is immediate. Players will lose a turn the first time.
2. The Spy order ignored its target id twice (Mosswen spied Leafroot and Bloodwells where she stood); the target form is a province id in the brief but the engine spies the hero's own province. Spy 3 also returned "yields little" on its own province.
3. leave_army with an index fails ("could not find specified armies"); a unit type name works. The brief says 'all'.
4. The recruit list shows "Wild Hunt Mammoth Host 5g" to a Divine realm (Pagan-only price shown to everyone).
5. The capital is "hallowed ground already" but its province line does not say consecrated, and it isn't counted in the consecrated total, and Blessed levies don't apply there. The Divine player can't tell what the capital gives.
6. No neighbour's defender count is shown anywhere; every attack on neutral land is blind unless a hero stands there. Three of the repelled attacks this game were blind.
7. Terror x3 shows on sea provinces.
8. Hire only works where the king (or hero) stands; the view lists wanderers "in your land" without saying who can hire them.
9. "Druid shortfall −40%" for the Wild Hunt's beasts and for the Evil king's Mammoth even with Beast-masters: the beast-command rule and its interaction with the kit perks is not explained anywhere a player sees.
10. The 'treat' diplomacy offer produced no line in any report.

## Three balance observations
1. **Evil's "casts down the altars" promotion is far too strong.** Five empty consecrated provinces turned an Average host into an all-Elite one for free, and Divine's Tithes make Divine the realm with the most empty consecrated land. Suggest: one random company gains a level (not all), or only when the province was defended.
2. **Call down the storm is under-priced at 4g**: 28g destroyed 16 companies (≈45g of recruits) and demoted 15 more, from safety, with no counter. Suggest 6–8g, or once every other turn, or cap the destroyed at 2.
3. **Divine's Tithes are great, Divine Power is unreachable**: with 7 consecrated provinces Divine still had Power 0 because it held no holy site, so none of the six gifts appeared in 16 turns. Either count a consecrated province as a point (or 2 consecrated = 1 point), or make holy sites visible/plentiful early. Also: Divine needs a cheap way to garrison what it consecrates, or the kit feeds Evil.
Also noted: Pagan's wage problem is self-inflicted but easy to fall into (cheap kindred wanderers at 2g); Blood Harvest and the storm are the realm's only money; the Rite of the Ley never came up in a 16-turn game.
