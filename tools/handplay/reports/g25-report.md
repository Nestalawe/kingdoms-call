## Each alignment, and the round-25 change

**Undead (first at turn 10, fifth at the end).** Cross-trained heroes carried it: Necromancy 3-4 after turn 1, 5 by turn 4, three skeletons a cast from the capital and Fairstead barrows, Bind to Crack by turn 9 (cheap: 2-26g a turn). The king's own casts at Necromancy 1-2 fizzled four of six. Skeletons at 1g for two made the realm absurdly rich: 246g at turn 10, 862g at the end, with nothing to spend it on. The real flaw showed from turn 11: skeletons must stay with a necromancer, so the Undead garrisoned nothing; three heroes were three hosts, and the Good walked into eleven empty provinces, the capital included, while the Undead king held one company. Bind's Crack cap never mattered. Lichdom was never needed (the king lived). Peace offers four turns running; the Good never answered.

**Good (R0 won; R9 fourth).** The Villages paid on most land and both realms finished with 600-1,100g. The Beacons were lit four times (12g each, 6 Veterans wage-free) and every muster went home with a line after three turns; R0's musters stood in conquered Undead land while its hosts moved on. Knights errant stopped doubling at Tactical 5; R9's king and R0's hero still reached 6 by battle. R9 was boxed in by Neutral and lost two hosts on the League capital's garrison. The pressed levy was R9's way to rebuild (33g for 8 companies).

**Pagan (R8 second; R1 eighth).** R8 woke six sites (Favour at ×1.5) and stormed eight times at 8g: never a province by itself, but it softened R2's capital three turns running (1-2 destroyed, 3-6 demoted each) until R8 took it on turn 19. Aelyroot's woken stones held against R2's 13 companies. R1 had stones in one province and lost its capital to an Undead walk-in on turn 11 (war without warning), then survived on peace. The Wild Hunt was refused three times: the stones must stand beside an unclaimed creature or monster province, and Pagan's own Scalepeak (a troll province) does not count.

**Evil (R7 third; R2 sixth).** Both had a monster province by turn 4 (Hydras; Elder Dragons), so the Dark Pact without one never came up. R2 made six pacts at 12g (one Hydra each, 12-13 HP), bred Hydras drew half wages, and it never ran short of money: the wage change works. The altar promotion fired twice when R2 walked into consecrated Bonegreen and Zogfield: one company each, a bonus now, not an engine. R2 took the Divine capital on turn 11 and then lost its own twice to Pagan storms and Elite companies. R7's three dragons and a Basilisk beat every host they met (the Druid king three times, the Neutral king three times) and took two capitals.

**Divine (eliminated turn 11).** Consecration worked and Power rose 1, 2, 3 (Sanctuary, Grace, Revelation) with the new report line each time, then fell 3 → 0 the turn Evil walked into consecrated Bonegreen. The Divine saw the Evil host beside Bonegreen (new sight rule) and marched to garrison it; Evil declared and walked in at phase 0, before the column arrived. A Proclaimed Crusade (×1.5 shown) did not save the king's 4 companies from 7 with 3 Hydras. Pressed levies (Veterans at a consecrated province) were bought twice; wages then disbanded 9 companies at 0g. Sanctuary healing never fired (no wounded Divine hero on holy ground).

**Druidic (eliminated turn 13).** Took two creature provinces (Stone Giant submitted), Grow the Wild twice, Call of the Wild pulled a Stone Giant for 2g. Then R7 Evil's dragons took four provinces in four turns. Grow the Wild on Redhold was too late: the forest comes at season's end, the attack came in phase 1.

**Neutral (seventh).** Trade Pacts bought Stoneweald (14g), Grommire (26g), and at peace R7's Thalridge (tax 10, 40g) and Elenwold (20g). The new refusals fired with their reasons: "another realm's hero stands in Elenwold" (twice), "a creature or monster province" (Savagespire, Fangdeep). The at-war and taken-this-turn refusals were never the first reason reached. R7 declared the next turn and took Thalridge back. Embargoes on the Undead and R7 (5g). Ransom: the Neutral king, at war with the Druids, ran down the landless Druid king with the ransom flag set and slew him (kings cannot be ransomed; nothing says so). A Sage 9 found thirty discoveries; 430g unspent at the end.

## Report and order problems (new ones)

1. **A king cannot be held to ransom**, and the Encounter with the ransom flag set ends in his death with no word why.
2. **The sight rule warns, but war without warning still lands first.** The Divine and Pagan R1 saw the Evil and Undead hosts beside their land; both declared and walked in at phase 0 before a single company could be moved in. The warning helps against realms whose wars wait a turn; against Evil and Undead it only shows what is about to happen.
3. **"The contest for Zogpeak lapses: Realm9 and Realm3 are no longer at war"**, when two realms at peace attacked the same neutral province in one phase; they had never been at war.
4. **A garrison rallies to any hero of its realm standing in the province** ("the standing troops of Faircairn rally to Sela"), so a lone hero in a capital doubles as a field army; nothing in the order form says so.
5. **The Trade Pact refusal checks the reasons in a fixed order**, so a province with a hero in it never reports that it was also taken last turn; fine, but the at-war rule cannot be seen at all on most borders (the hero or creature check comes first).
6. **The storm's target rule is still unstated**: "only on a province holding stones the realm has woken, or one beside such a province" appears only when refused.
7. **Wild Hunt refusals do not say that the realm's own creature province does not count.**
8. **Pressed levies of another race under a king without Druid read "Druid shortfall −20%"** (R8's Orc and Elven companies under a human king, R9's humans under an elf).

## Three balance observations

1. **The Undead cannot spend or garrison.** 862g at the end, one company with the king, eleven empty provinces walked into. Suggest: skeletons may stand as a garrison in a province holding a barrow or a blighted province (the dead of this soil), and Bind the Bones may also lift garrison skeletons; or let the Undead hire living garrisons at a discount. Keep the 1g-for-two wage, it is not the problem; idleness is.
2. **War without warning beats the new sight rule.** Seeing the host next door gave the defender one phase that it never got. Suggest: a realm declaring war without warning cannot attack in the first phase of that turn (phase 0), so a defender who sees the host has phase 0 to move in; or the declaration takes effect at phase 1.
3. **Treasuries still pile up** (seven realms over 170g, three over 580g at the end) even with pressed levies used by every realm. Suggest: allow the pressed levy twice a turn per province at ×2 and ×3, or let gold buy a tier for a garrison company (a drill at 5g), so a rich realm can turn gold into defence.
