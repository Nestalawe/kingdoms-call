# Game g2: Good v Neutral v Undead (seed 202, round 23 kits, 16 turns, all three realms hand-played)

## Result
- **Turn 8:** Undead 5 provinces (42g, 15 companies, all Crack), Good 5 provinces (2g, wages above tax), Neutral 5 provinces (34g). Undead had declared war on Good on turn 7 and taken Skullmesa; the Good had just held Redreach.
- **Turn 16 (end):** Undead 10 provinces, 47 of the world's 197 tax (24%), 126g, a 14-company Elite skeletal host, wages 20g. Good 5 provinces, 33 tax, 17g, field army destroyed, king alive. Neutral 2 provinces, 8 tax, 1g, one company. The Reckoning threshold is falling 5% a turn (40% at the end); the Undead reaches it in a few turns. **Undead wins.**

## What each kit did
**Undead (won).**
- *Harvest the Field* (2g a grave, one a phase): used almost every turn, 20+ times. After every battle, won or lost, the king raised the dead of both sides on the spot: his own fallen Dwarven Guard, the Good's Wyvern Riders and Elite Shieldwall, Neutral's 1g mercenary bands, the Mammoth Hosts of Elderridge. At barrows (Kazwells, Aelydelve) it is a 6g-a-turn company factory (3 graves refill each turn). Skeletal companies destroyed leave no graves, so the host's own losses cost nothing to the enemy either.
- *Bind the Bones* (2g a company, once a turn): used 4 times (14g, 22g, 20g, 16g). Took every skeletal company in the king's province up a tier each time: Average → Veteran → Crack → Elite over three turns. The best gold-to-strength order in the game by a wide margin; a 22g bind turned 221 strength into 335. Elite companies are skipped, so the cost falls as the host matures.
- *The dead fear nothing*: ×1.5 → ×1.7 as blighted provinces were added. It is the whole reason the host was unbeatable: 16 skeletal companies at ×1.65 under the king. Under a plain hero the garrisons got only "+30% know no fear", and unled they fought at plain strength and were swept away by 5 Good companies (Kazwells, Dunfell, Goldheight all fell in two turns). So the Undead is one host, not an empire: everything away from the king is weak.
- *Blight the Land* (3g): used 4 times (Kazwells, Goldheight, Skullmesa, Starmeadow). Tax 4 → 1 + 4g grave goods + a free Average garrison company a turn (to 4): good on tax-4 land, pointless on tax-6 Goldheight. The income vanished the moment the Good took the provinces (12g → 4g), and blighted land is worth 1 tax to its conqueror for ever. The free garrisons cannot be led away ("the dead of this soil will not leave it"), which is right, but they also hold nothing.
- *Undead start* (2 skeletal companies): with Harvest at the home barrow the king led 7 companies on turn 3 and 10 on turn 5.
- Unused: Skeletal Armies spell (Harvest made it redundant), Desecrate (no Divine).
- *Hero-led fear only*: Nyssa with 2 living companies + 4 skeletal garrison got "+30%" not ×1.65. Worth confirming that is intended; the kit text says "under the king or a hero".

**Good (second).**
- *Shield of the Realm* ×1.75 on own ground + *Defend as a standing watch* (heroes in neighbouring provinces march in): held Redreach on turn 8 against 335 strength with 228 (no losses, 6 promotions). This, with Tactical 7-9, is the Good's real kit and it is good.
- *Reclaim* ×1.5 fired when retaking Skullmesa: fine.
- *The Villages* (+3 tax on humanoid tax ≤ 4): quietly worth 6-9g a turn; the Good's tax went 23 → 65 at nine provinces.
- *Light the Beacons* (2g a Veteran company, every 3 turns): used 3 times, 13 Veteran garrison companies in all. **It is a trap as written.** Each Beacon company costs 2g once and then about 3g a turn for ever; the second Beacons pushed wages from 35g to 52g against 40g tax, the third from 45g to 64g against 48g. The Good realm spent turns 8-11 at 0-2g and disbanded 6 good companies by hand. A human would still press it, because the garrisons do win defensive battles; the order needs its wage tail stated, or Beacon companies should serve wage-free for a few turns and then disband (a muster, not a standing army).
- *Good Works* (free): run 6 times, cleared nothing (no Terror or plague in this match). Harmless, no value here.
- *Call to Arms*: not used.
- Unused by design: nothing in the kit answers a stronger attacker. Once the Undead host was Elite at ×1.65, the Good's 5-8 companies at ×1.75 lost every attack and, holding, won once and lost twice (the Undead's own-capital ×1.5 decided Dunfell).
- *Feigned Retreat* (Tactical 7) laid the same phase as the attack showed no +25% line in the battle; *Grand Stratagem* (Tactical 9) was set a phase late by me.

**Neutral (third, collapsed).**
- *Trade Pact* (4g a tax point + 3g a defender): Starmeadow (tax 2) for 11g on turn 3, Anvilflats (tax 4, 3 defenders) for 25g on turn 10. Both bought without a fight and the defenders became the garrison. Horndeep (jungle) was refused: "needs an unclaimed humanoid province here or next door" (the message should say "Horndeep is a creature province"). Buying Anvilflats produced no line in the realm's own report (only the GM log). Good value but slow: 25g is a turn's tax.
- *The bands come to the League / hires best* (1g a band, one tier up): three bands hired at Dawnscar on turn 7 for 3g, Condottieri ×1.5. The League's best trick. But the two Wyla lost at Elderridge were harvested by the Undead the same turn.
- *Embargo* (5g): −20% tax on the Undead for 2 turns, about 6g in all. Poor value against a 40g treasury.
- *Ransom*: never happened. Duels in the harness have no stance, and the only king duel (Undead king on Neutral king, turn 12) was a kill.
- *Broker*: "requires a target — a hero to hire or a province to buy word from" with a hero id as target; the format is unclear from the brief.
- Neutral's problem was not the kit. It had the second economy at turn 8 (34g, 40 tax) and lost 7 companies to a wage shortfall over three turns after the capital fell; the "rawest troops melt away first" rule disbands the garrisons too. A neighbour with a ×1.65 host and no truce is unwinnable for a realm whose kit is money and mercenaries.

## Strong, weak, fun
- **Strong:** the Undead loop Harvest → Bind → march is the strongest thing I have seen in 23 rounds. Bots never stack the three orders in one turn, which is why the matrix shows the Undead at 14%. Hand-played, it compounded from turn 3 and was out of reach by turn 8. Its gold efficiency is absurd: 2g for an Average company off any corpse, 2g to raise a tier, 1g upkeep each, and the king's Host-Leader trait cut 10-16g of wages a turn.
- **Weak:** Good's Beacons (wage trap); Neutral's Embargo; Undead blight on anything above tax 4; Good Works without Terror or plague about.
- **Fun:** the Good's turn-8 stand at Redreach (every defensive rule firing at once), the turn-13 raid that took the Undead capital with five companies while the host was four hexes away, the duel that killed the Neutral king, the Undead king's 4-battle grind at Goldheight on turn 16 (364 v 124 → 26, the last Elite Shieldwall refusing to yield). The hex map made all three strategic (the three realms met at Elderridge/Hornshaw on turn 6).

## Report and order-format problems
1. `search` by lair name is ignored: the engine wants the feature index on the province's full feature list (cleared lairs included). Cost one hero (Brackthorn) in g2's early turns.
2. Harness submits no duel stance: kings "will not answer the call" and fought at −20% in every battle; no Ransom was possible.
3. Hiring a hero prints its line twice (Mira turn 6, Requia turn 10).
4. Trade Pact success line reaches only the GM log, not the realm's report (turn 10).
5. `disband_army` by type takes the first match (Elaria lost a Crack company she meant to keep); needs a way to pick the company.
6. Mercenary hire syntax: "merc:<id>" was read as a unit name ("Shieldwall Levies not raised here"); a bare id gave "entirely blocked: ." with an empty reason.
7. War declared by a living realm takes effect at the end of the turn, the Undead's at once ("the dead keep no truce"). Fair, but the first turn's "turned back at the border" surprised me; the report could say when the war begins.
8. Peace: both sides must send 'treat' the same turn; the Undead's attacks that turn still landed. Fine, but a line "peace from next turn" would help.
9. Duel narration is two lines with no rolls or stats.
10. The Beacons text should state the wage tail; the Blight text should say blighted land stays tax 1 for its conqueror.

## Three balance observations
1. **Undead needs a brake on Harvest + Bind.** Options: Bind the Bones once per company per game (or only Average → Veteran), or Harvest the Field limited to the loser's dead, or hero-led fear capped at ×1.5 with blight adding nothing to the host. Any one of these; the loop is three multipliers on one host.
2. **Good's Beacons should be a muster, not a payroll.** Beacon companies wage-free for 3 turns and then dismissed (or 1g a turn): the Good would then light them before a fight, as the fiction says, instead of bankrupting itself.
3. **A capital's fall should hurt the attacker's reach, not just the owner.** Unled skeletal garrisons at plain strength meant 5 Good companies took 4 Undead provinces in two turns, and the Undead king retook them in two more because nothing can hold ground except a king's host. A garrison bonus for the dead of this soil (say ×1.3 unled) would make blight a defence; a smaller fear multiplier would make the Good's gains stick. Either, not both.
