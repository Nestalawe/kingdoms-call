**Pagan wins** on tax share and gold; Evil (Orcish) holds the most land. No realm came near the Reckoning. The Undead finished last: they lost their capital twice to Pagan storms and their king to a duel.

## Each alignment

**Undead (last).** Without Harvest the Field the opening was a dice game. The king's own Skeletal Armies runs on his trained Necromancy (1, so 40%) and fizzled in roughly ten of twelve casts all game; the barrow and signature bonuses add companies per cast, not odds. The two heroes practised Necromancy 1 to 3 in a turn, reached 80% by turn 5, and raised almost every company the realm had. Bind to Crack worked as written: 8 to 12g a turn kept nine companies at Crack; nothing ever reached Elite by binding. The host never snowballed: 10 to 13 companies all game, against 15 to 24 in the hand-played games with Harvest. Lichdom brought the king back at the barrow with 9 HP and nothing. The change did what it was meant to; the Undead are now too weak in this game, mainly because a king with Necromancy 1 is useless at the one thing the kit needs.

**Pagan (won).** Six woken sites by turn 13, Favour ×1.5 from turn 9, The Old Stones ×1.6 broke two Undead attacks with no loss, Blood Harvest paid about 50g. The storm at 8g was cast eight times; results ran from nothing (0 destroyed, 2 demoted) to decisive (4 of 9 destroyed at Morleaf, then a walk-in). At 8g it was still the order that decided every Pagan conquest after turn 9, but it was no longer cast every turn by reflex. The treasury was the problem, not the kit: 625g and nothing to spend it on.

**Divine.** The best demonstration of the round-24 Divine Power change. With one holy site and 11 consecrations, Power rose 2 → 3 (turn 5) → 4 (turn 9) → 5 (turn 11) → 6 (turn 14): all six gifts. But none of them did anything I could see: no report line ever mentions Sanctuary, Revelation, Pilgrim roads, Absolution or the Church Triumphant, no Divine hero was attacked, and the realm had no Terror or plague to clear. Tithes paid 31g of 100g tax by the end. Crusade against the wild ×1.5 won every creature province. Divine was never attacked, so the altar change had nothing to test.

**Evil (two realms).** Dominion of the Pits plus Savage Fury chained Manticores and Cave Trolls at every monster province; the Orcish king made four Dark Pacts (−7 HP each) and ended with the most land (17). Its one defeat was the war on Good: 9 companies with 3 Manticores lost 4 against the Shield ×1.75, after a Good Works order lifted three Terror stacks the same phase. The Human Evil realm's necromancer hero raised 13 skeletal companies at a barrow, lost 5 of them attacking two Crack skeletal Undead companies, then rebuilt with chained Manticores. Neither Evil realm ever captured consecrated land, so the one-company altar promotion never fired.

**Good (two realms).** The Shield held the only attack on a Good capital (above). The Beacons muster went home after three turns with a clear line both times it was lit; the second one (12g, six companies) came on turn 15 and will go home after turn 18. The Villages on tax ≤5 paid on most of both realms' early provinces; Good (Human) ended with 418g. Good (Elven) was the poorest realm all game (0g at turns 7 and 8, one company disbanded) because two early defeats cost it half its army; the Beacons' wage-free muster was the only thing that kept its capital defended. Hearts of Oak saved a hero from a lair twice.

**Neutral (two realms).** Trade Pacts bought ten provinces between them (14 to 48g each). The round-24 refusals fired correctly twice (a hero of the owner standing there), but the message is one long sentence listing every reason and does not say which applied. The Dwarven realm hoarded 511g and lost four provinces in two turns once Evil declared war (immediate) and walked into ungarrisoned bought land. Embargo worked (−20% tax). Ransom was never tested: I set no Neutral hero to chase a duel. Monster mercenary bands hired by a hero with Druid 0 went to the garrison.

**Druidic.** Parley with the Wild ×1.5 and "the pack submits" took every Thunderbird province; the wild sent a free Thunderbird garrison most turns. Poor all game (13g at turn 8), lost two provinces to Neutral walk-ins during a war the Neutral realm declared.

## Report and order problems (new ones)

1. **Divine Power and its gifts are never reported.** The player sees "the realm's Divine Power is 2" once, in the consecration line, and nothing after. No line says a gift was gained, lost or used. I added a Divine Power line to my harness view (additive, scratch only); the portal needs one.
2. **Skeletal Armies uses only trained Necromancy for its chance** (the line shows "Necromancy 3 — 1 trained, +2 from items: 40%"). A player reading "Necromancy 3" expects 3's odds.
3. **The Trade Pact refusal names every possible reason in one sentence.** It should say which one applied (here: another realm's hero stands there).
4. **A storm's report line to the victim is good**, but the caster's line does not say which companies were hit.
5. **A peace overture "went unanswered this year"** in the same turn it was sent, before the other side could answer (seen twice).
6. **A king on Defend rides out to a neighbour's battle** (the Pagan king left Ashmarch to hold Oldstone): good play, but nothing warns the player that it empties the province he leaves.
7. **Hiring a monster band with a Druid-0 hero** sends it to the garrison with a line, but the recruit list does not warn first.
8. **Recruit in a province that cannot raise the type** fails with "not raised here" though the hero chose it from the capital's list a phase earlier; the list should be per province.

Harness: helper scripts in the scratchpad only (s16.py with a Divine Power line, v16.sh, turn16.sh, stand16.py); nothing in the repository changed.

## Three balance observations

1. **The Undead now need a working necromancer king.** Without Harvest, a king with Necromancy 1 (40%, one cast a turn) cannot raise his own army; the realm depends on two heroes and fell from first to last. Suggest: the Undead king starts at Necromancy 2 (plus the signature), and a cast at a held barrow counts the barrow bonus in its chance (or never fizzles). Keep Harvest removed and Bind to Crack.
2. **Divine Power from consecrations works, but the gifts are invisible and mostly inert.** Power 6 was reached by turn 14 with one holy site, which is perhaps too easy; and the gifts did nothing visible in a game where nobody attacked Divine. Suggest: three consecrations per point (not two), and give each gift a report line when gained and when it acts.
3. **Gold piles up with nowhere to go.** Pagan 625g, Neutral 566g and 511g, Good 418g at turn 16. Wars were few because a ten-realm map has room to expand until turn 12. Suggest a gold sink that buys power: a larger recruit cap per province, or kit orders that scale with spending (a storm or a Beacons muster at a higher tier for more gold). Separately, the storm at 8g felt right: keep it.
