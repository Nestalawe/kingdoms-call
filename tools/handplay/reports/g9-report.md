# g9 report: Divine (Human) v Neutral (Dwarven) v Pagan (Elven) v Undead (Orcish), seed 909, sea 20%

Hand-played, all four realms by me, round 23 kits, 16 turns.

## Result

| | Turn 8 | Turn 16 |
|---|---|---|
| Divine | 4 provinces, 14g, tax 32 (about 10%) | **0 provinces**, 56g, three heroes alive and landless |
| Neutral | 4 provinces, 12g, tax 24 (about 8%) | 7 provinces, 117g, tax 52 (about 20%) |
| Pagan | 5 provinces, 51g, tax 38 (about 12%) | 7 provinces, 41g, tax 48 (about 18%) |
| Undead | 4 provinces, 12g, tax 27 (about 9%) | **16 provinces, 172g, tax 94 (26% of the world's tax base)** |

The Undead won, and wiped Divine off the map in five turns. By turn 8 the Undead king led 19 companies raised for 2g each from two barrows that refilled every turn; by turn 11 he had bound them all to Crack and Elite (40g a time) and marched 24 companies into Divine's Wildpass. Divine's ×1.75 Crusade against the Undead, holy defence and a Tactical 4 king lost 468 to 181, then 794 to 141 at the capital, then 1079 to 159 and 857 to 66. Every consecrated province fell blighted. Pagan woke four sites by turn 9 (Favour at the ×1.5 cap) and took Oldreach's holy site from the League by storm, but its 8-company assault on the Neutral capital lost half its strength and it sat out the last four turns at its stones. Neutral spent turns 3 to 9 at 2–12g from the wages of what it bought and hired, then held its capital, bought back its land, embargoed Pagan three times and ended the game with the second treasury. The crown's price fell to 40% on turn 15; the Undead needed a few turns more.

## Divine

**Used:** Consecrate (five provinces), Tithes (+13g a turn at its peak), Blessed levies (Orc Berserkers and Black Arrows mustered as Veterans at Urukfield), Crusade against the wild (×1.5 on every creature province: Unicorns, Great Bears), Holy defence and Crusade against the Undead (×1.75 in all four battles), Proclaim Crusade (turn 12, honoured for three turns), Hounds of God (tried twice), Last Rites (fired at Wildpass, Urukfield and Elderglade: the Undead raised nothing from those fields), Bless Province (White 5: one fizzle, then +80% tax at the capital the turn it fell).
**Mattered:** Tithes and Consecrate made Divine the second economy by turn 10 (51g tax on 6 provinces, 54g in hand). Crusade against the wild carried every early conquest. Nothing in the kit mattered against the Undead host.
**Never found a use for:** Divine Power (Divine held no holy site all game: the one within reach, Oldreach, was Neutral's and then Pagan's; so no Sanctuary, Grace abounding, Revelation, Pilgrim roads, Absolution or Church Triumphant), Divine Grace (no Divine hero died), Hounds of God (a hero cannot run down a king, Crusade or not; and the sentries of an 18-company host turned Seraphine away from Barrowen, so the "always catches him" never reached a duel), Holy wrath (never in a duel).
**Strong:** the money. Divine's tax per province was the best on the map while the land was held.
**Weak:** the kit has no answer to a host three times its size, and every bonus it gets on consecrated ground is the Undead's gain when the ground falls (Desecrate blights it: tax 1 for ever, a free skeletal garrison a turn, +4g a turn to the Undead). Five consecrations cost 25g and became five blighted provinces. Divine Power sits behind holy sites that are rare and often in someone else's land.
**Fun:** the first ten turns (duel kills by the king, consecrating outward, the economy). The last five turns were watching the inevitable; the one bright moment was the landless king cutting the Undead king to 1 HP in the final duel.

## Neutral

**Used:** Trade Pact (Oldreach 18g on turn 3 with its holy site; Grimverge 26g on turn 14; refused on Pagan-held Oldreach: "needs an unclaimed humanoid province"), The League hires best (six bands hired at 1–2g each, Condottieri ×1.5), Open ranks (an Elven company led at ×1.25 but still "Druid shortfall −20%"), Embargo (three times, 5g each: −8g a turn to Pagan for six turns), Guilds (Corin, Spy 4 → 5: Grishvale and Ruindeep revealed), Markets, Quick Learners, Deserters turn mercenary (the Wargs of Grishvale stood as three 1g bands at the capital after the last battle), Research (Yarrow Sage 2 → 9: twenty-odd discoveries in sixteen turns, including Dragon Taming and Horse Traders).
**Mattered:** holding the capital on turn 12 (×1.5 home ground, 143 against 237, no losses, 4 of 8 Pagan companies destroyed) and the three Embargoes. Trade Pacts bought two provinces without a fight, but each came with a garrison the League then paid for.
**Never found a use for:** Ransom (no encounter plan field reaches the engine for a human player; the only Neutral duels were the king's, and he killed), Broker (never tried: the brief's format is unclear and the king's five orders were always full), Assassinate (needs Spy 9, not 5), Steal (Corin had Spy, not Thief).
**Strong:** research with a Neutral Sage (×2 discovery chance: a pair of finds almost every phase from Sage 5 on), cheap bands, the Embargo.
**Weak:** wages. From turn 3 to turn 9 the League had 2–12g: Oldreach's two Noble Griffons cost 8g a turn against 18g tax, three hired bands took wages to 31g on 25g tax, and disbanding a garrison company takes three orders (pick up, disband, leave). Trade Pact needs an unclaimed humanoid neighbour; most of the League's neighbours were monster provinces (three Elder Dragons at Ruindeep) or someone's already.
**Fun:** the capital stand, the research stream, the pact haggling. The middle game was waiting for gold.

## Pagan

**Used:** Rite of Awakening (four sites by turn 9: Talongreen, Faeheight, Moonroot, Silverdune; +3 tax each, Favour ×1.16 → ×1.5), Favour of the Old Gods (on every battle line), Blood Harvest (+2g a kill and a promotion nearly every time: one Forest Wardens company went Average → Elite by turn 7 on promotions alone), Call down the storm (five times, 4g each: 5 companies destroyed and 4 demoted in all, and it emptied Oldreach so the king walked in), Rite of the Wild Hunt (twice: two Veteran Manticores for 5g, wage-free, two turns each; both bindings ended before a battle), Rite of the Ley (three walks between Talongreen and Moonroot, instant), Blood Offering (one Green company for one promotion to Veteran: poor value, it should be two tiers), the king's duels (three hero kills).
**Mattered:** Awakening and Favour; the storm on Oldreach; Blood Harvest's promotions.
**Never found a use for:** The ancestors watch (no Pagan hero died in play; Kaine died to a boar on turn 4 and nothing in the view showed a watch), The Old Stones ×1.6 (Pagan was never attacked at a stones province). A rite and the storm both count as "the stones have answered once this turn": the Ley walk out of Moonroot blocked the king's Blood Offering there the same turn.
**Strong:** a woken-stones economy and a ×1.5 host by turn 9; the storm at 4g.
**Weak:** attacking a capital: 237 at Favour ×1.5 lost to 143 on home ground ×1.5 with the Forest Wardens silencing the archers. Pagan wages ran level with tax from turn 11 (39g on 38g under Embargo). The Wild Hunt's beasts expire before a two-turn march reaches a fight.
**Fun:** the rites and the storm; the duel kills; the Ley walk is a lovely trick.

## Undead

**Used:** Harvest the Field (about thirty times: 2g a company, three a turn at each humanoid barrow, plus the dead of every field), The barrow dead (Kroncopse, Wendstead, Gnafen: three graves back every turn), Bind the Bones (seven times, 12g to 48g a time; it binds as many as the gold allows, no refusal), The dead fear nothing (×1.5 → ×1.7 as the blighted count rose), Skeletal Armies (Barrowen at Necromancy 3 + 3 from barrows: six casts, two fizzles, three companies a success), Desecrate (five consecrated provinces blighted on capture), Grave Goods (+20g a turn by the end), The dead of this soil (a free skeletal garrison every turn in each of the five blighted provinces), the betrayal war (declared and marched the same turn), Raiders' Muster, Host-Leader (−30g of wages a turn).
**Mattered:** all of it, in this order: barrows → Harvest → Bind → fear. Two heroes only (Ossric died to a Weak lair on turn 2), and the second host of 18 Gyrocopters took three provinces on its own.
**Never found a use for:** Blight the Land (never needed: Desecrate blighted five provinces for free), The soul is taken (no Divine hero died), Lichdom (the king never fell).
**Strong:** everything compounds, and the money is absurd: 172g in hand at the end with 44 companies, because skeletons cost 1g and the Host-Leader halves the bill.
**Weak:** the only loss all game was Barrowen's 10 Gyrocopters in a forest against 2 Black Arrows (forest −25%, a refused duel −20%, Lightning Bolt and two volleys). The Undead king is the realm: landless King0 cut him to 1 HP in the last duel; had he died with no barrow in Divine land, Lichdom would have carried him home.
**Fun:** as the Undead, every turn was a win from turn 3. As any neighbour, there was nothing to do after turn 10.

## Report and order problems

1. **A hero cannot run down a king** ("only a king may run down another king"), so the Crusade's Hounds of God never applies to the enemy king, and a lone challenger is "turned away by the sentries" of any hero leading a host. The kit text promises a duel the engine never grants.
2. **Assassinate needs Spy 9** ("lacks the Spy mastery (9)"); the brief said 5. Steal needs Thief, not Spy. The view should say both.
3. **Recruit takes one company per order**, whatever the `|qty`; and two heroes cannot recruit the same unit type in the same province in one turn ("already recruited this turn"). Neither is in the order form.
4. **One rite per province per turn includes the Rite of the Ley**: Vervain walking out of Moonroot at phase 0 blocked the king's Blood Offering at Moonroot at phase 1 ("the stones have already answered once this turn"). The Ley's text does not say it spends the stones' rite.
5. **Trade Pact on another realm's province is refused** ("needs an unclaimed humanoid province") although the kit text says "whether it is unclaimed or another realm's".
6. **A research pick left unsent is chosen by the scholars** ("No choice was sent"): fine, but five new pairs a turn at Sage 5+ with Neutral ×2 means a human must answer five questions every turn; a "keep the best of each category" default would help.
7. **The hallowed capital** is not counted among consecrated provinces, is not desecrated when it falls, and gives no Tithes: the view never says what the capital's hallowing does.
8. **Hiring prints the hero's acceptance twice** (Corin, Vervain).
9. **Disbanding a garrison company** takes pickup, disband, leave; `disband_army` from a hero with no armies says "no armies to disband".
10. **A hero alone can walk into an unclaimed province with defenders and spy it** (Corin at Grishvale and Ruindeep) but "can only observe": the view should say a lone hero is safe there.
11. **Blood Offering gave one tier** ("rise to Veteran") for a company sacrificed; the kit says two.
12. **The Wild Hunt needs a creature province "nearby"** but Moonroot, two hexes from Talongreen's Manticores, was refused; the radius is not stated.
13. The harness: `discoveryChoices` keyed by the pair's `key` works (the g4 addition); no harness change was needed this game.

## Three balance observations

1. **The Undead loop has no counter the Divine kit can reach.** Harvest at 2g from barrows that refill three graves a turn, Bind the Bones with no cap but gold, and the fear multiplier made a 468-strength host by turn 11 against a Divine host of 181 that was using every bonus it owns (×1.75, Tactical 4, consecrated ground). Then Desecrate paid the Undead for every consecration Divine had bought. Suggest, together: Bind the Bones once per company per game, or capped at Crack; a captured barrow refills one grave a turn (three only at the capital barrow); and the Last Rites should also stop Desecrate's free garrison for the first few turns, or Desecrate should not blight a province the Divine realm retakes within 5 turns.
2. **Divine Power is unreachable on an ordinary map.** Two games running Divine held no holy site in 16 turns; six gifts sat unseen. Count every two consecrated provinces as one point of Power (so five consecrations reach Sanctuary and Grace abounding), or seed one holy site within two hexes of every Divine capital.
3. **The League's wage curve needs a kink, not more gold.** Bought garrisons at full wages and hired bands at 1–2g put Neutral at 2–12g for seven turns; a human would do the same. Bought defenders could serve at half wages (they are paid already), and the Embargo, the League's one weapon, could be 3g.

Also noted: Weak lairs kill heroes with Melee 0–1 (Ossric to a Clay Sentinel, Kaine to a Wild Boar); Pagan's storm again decided a province for 4g; a capital's ×1.5 home ground with Forest Wardens beat a ×1.5 Favour host, so Pagan's cap is not the whole story.
