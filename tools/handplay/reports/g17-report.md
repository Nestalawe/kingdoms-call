## Each alignment, and how the round-24 changes played

**Evil (R0 won, R7 third).** Spread Dread on turn 1 put Terror ×3 on every neighbour, and R0 took a province a turn without a loss. Two monster pits gave chained hydras and basilisks (Dominion, the pits yield, Savage Fury). Dark Pact ran every other turn at 12g and 6 HP. The war on R9 was declared on turn 6 and took effect at once: King0 walked into empty Goldhold the same turn. R7 had no monster province until turn 8. Its Dark Pact on Stone Giants was refused ("the pits are empty": giants are creatures). It then took Cave Troll pits and grew to the most provinces by walking into R0's bare provinces behind R0's front. **The altar promotion never fired.** No Evil host came near consecrated land: the Divine realm was on the far side of the board.

**Pagan (R2 second, R8 seventh).** R2 woke seven sites: Favour at the cap by turn 8, The Old Stones ×1.6 on defence. **The storm at 8g** was cast six times and was worth it every time: 1 destroyed of 4 at Starthicket, 1+1 at Wyrmsands, 2 destroyed and 3 demoted at Eastvale, 1+3 at Caelleaf, 0+2 at Caelscar. Each softened a defender the king then beat. At 8g it is a once-a-war tool, not every turn, which feels right. R8 never woke more than one site but beat the R6 Druid king twice in a row on Blood Harvest money. Wild Hunt, Blood Offering and the Ley were never needed.

**Undead (fourth).** **Skeletal Armies alone was enough.** The king's two starting skeletons plus 14 companies raised from battlefields (5 fizzles in all; two Necromancy 2 casters) made a Crack skeletal host by turn 7. It took Highdrift from the Good king on turn 10. **Bind the Bones to Crack bit at once.** Three binds and then "no skeletal company here can be bound any tighter" from turn 9 to the end. Without Harvest the Undead grew like any realm, not like a wall: 11 provinces at turn 8, 13 at the end. Then the Good king's Beacons garrisons and Forest Wardens beat it twice: 4 of 7 lost attacking Silroot in forest. The living host under Vaunt died to a Beacons garrison (6 of 6). Two blights, 8 dead-of-this-soil garrison companies, and 309g unspent at the end with nothing to buy. Desecrate, Lichdom and The soul is taken never came up.

**Good (fifth).** **The Beacons muster worked as designed.** It was lit four times for 12g each: six wage-free Veterans, and every muster went home on schedule with a line. It decided the Undead war: the musters held Deepsands and Silroot, and Vaunt's six-company host died against one. The king reached Tactical 8 from battles and retook Highdrift from the Undead host. **The Villages on tax ≤5** paid on most provinces, but nothing in the report names it (no "Villages" line all game). ## Report and order problems (new ones)

1. **Divine Power and its gifts are silent.** Power 9 and six gifts came with no line anywhere. The harness view's Power line is the only way to know.
2. **Ransom needs a lone target.** An encounter on a hero leading armies became an ordinary battle, so the Ransom flag did nothing. Nothing tells the player this.
3. **A realm can pay a ransom only automatically.** There was no choice or prompt: the Divine treasury paid 9g at the start of the turn.
4. **A landless enemy king and his heroes can stand in a province you own indefinitely**, with no line about it, until you Encounter them. King9 sat in Grimspire for three turns after it fell.
5. **Dark Pact on creatures is refused with "the pits are empty"** even though the realm held a Stone Giant province. The message should say the pact takes monsters only.
6. **The Villages never print a line** (unlike Tithes or healers' fees), so a Good player cannot tell what it earns.
7. **Bind the Bones' cap is reported only as a refusal.** The Undead king spent four turns' orders learning that every company was already Crack.
8. **"Druid shortfall −20%" again** printed for an Undead hero's living Orcish, Human and goblin host, and a Dwarven hero's dwarves.
9. **A third realm's march is "turned back at the border"** when the target province changes hands earlier in the same turn (R2 into Wyrmsands, turn 8).

## Three balance observations

1. **Divine Power from consecrations now overshoots.** With two holy sites, eight consecrations and a Crusade the realm had Power 9, every gift by turn 13. Suggest every *three* consecrated provinces count a point (not two), and the Crusade point only while at war. And give each gift a report line when it is gained or lost.
2. **The Evil doom-host still wins wars of conquest; the Druids are its prey.** Terror −30%, chained monsters and Savage Fury ×1.5 beat two Druidic realms whose bonus lives only in forest. R0 lost nothing all game. Suggest Terror fades a stack every 2 turns (not 3) on provinces the Evil realm does not border. Or give the Druids a plains answer: The beasts are yours ×1.25 when defending any province that holds a creature company.
3. **Undead without Harvest is now a mid-table realm with gold it cannot spend.** The bind cap and the slower casts worked: the host stopped at 7–9 Crack companies and lost to a Good Beacons line. But it ended with 309g. Suggest letting Bind the Bones also lift Average garrison skeletons in blighted provinces (to Veteran), so the gold has a defensive use. Leave the rest as it is.
