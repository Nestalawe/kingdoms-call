## Each alignment, and the round-27 changes

Both heroes practised Necromancy from turn 1 (one from nothing to 3 in a turn) and raised two or three a cast: 10 skeletons under a hero by turn 4, three barrows held by turn 12. Bind the Bones was used nine times (about 190g) and stopped at Crack. Four blights raised 19 unled garrison skeletons that stood all game; nobody attacked them. The realm won by walk-ins, not battles: it lost at Bromroot, and three times at the Pagan capital, but took about 15 empty provinces from R8 and Pagan. The altar promotion never arose. 847g unspent.

## Report and order problems (new)

1. **Ransom after a won battle did not fire in three chances.** (a) Turn 11: the League king destroyed an Evil hero's whole host, but the hero had "nowhere to retreat", a branch the rule does not cover; he walked free. (b) Turn 15: a League hero repelled an Evil hero who lost 1 company of 3: not half. (c) Turn 16: a "decisive rout" in which the beaten hero lost no company at all. Losers who retreat rarely lose half their companies.
2. **The bare-capital warning never fired, and three capitals fell.** R8's capital emptied during the turn (Defend rode both hosts out to a neighbour) and fell the same turn; R9's had a four-company garrison and fell to an instant war; Pagan's held a host. The warning's test (no garrison, no hero) is too narrow.
3. **A repelled king's later moves run from where he retreated to.** The Undead king, thrown back from Bromroot, carried out "move W" from his retreat province and walked into R8's empty capital. It decided that war.
4. **Planned moves beyond a host's reach are dropped without a line** (third and fourth moves of three kings on turns 2, 4 and 11).
5. **A storm's line to its victim still names no company** (the caster's now does).
6. **Landless realms linger.** Three kings with no land stood inside enemy provinces for nine turns, sending peace overtures nobody could usefully answer; no line says a realm is out.
7. **Divine Power's line lists what counts but not the cap**; on turn 8 it read 6 with 9 points earned.

## Three balance observations

1. **Walk-ins and the wage spiral decide wars; battles do not.** Every destroyed realm fell the same way: it lost empty provinces, its tax fell below its wages, and its army disbanded in two or three turns (R5 52g of wages on 32g; R8 49g on 45g; Pagan 72g on 68g). Suggest: an ungarrisoned province raises one free Green militia company when an enemy host enters, so a walk-in is at least a battle, and unpaid companies go home as garrisons at half wages for a turn before they disband.
2. **The Undead still run away when a barrow is next door.** Three barrows refilling three graves a turn, heroes at Necromancy 5 by turn 5, and 1g skeletons gave 55 companies and 847g. Suggest: only the barrow nearest the capital refills three graves a turn; every other held barrow refills one.
3. **Ransom needs a looser test.** Suggest: when the League wins a battle against a hero-led host and that host loses at least one company or is driven from the province, the hero is taken; cover the "nowhere to retreat" case too. As written it would have fired zero times in this game's 20 turns.

Also seen: the defender's swing again (16 Crusaders lost 11 to 8 defenders; 14 League companies lost 8 to two Titans). The storm at two destroyed and four demoted, and the pact's third-order seal, both felt right.
