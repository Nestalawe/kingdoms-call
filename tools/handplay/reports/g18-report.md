## Each alignment, and its round-24 change

**Undead.** Weakest realm on the board. Skeletal Armies alone did not build a host: King0 (Necromancy 2 trained) fizzled 4 of 6 casts, and the two heroes trained to Necromancy 4–5 fizzled about half of theirs too. With no barrow held in 16 turns (none was in reach), the only graves were battlefields, and those emptied after one cast. The skeletal host never passed 7 companies; a single neutral Dread Hydra threw it back from Blightmire three times. Blight paid (two blights: +8g grave goods, 4–5 free skeletal garrison companies each), but wages (35g of skeletons and living troops) ate everything and the realm ended at 0g. Bind to Crack fired once at the end (4g, two companies). **Round-24 verdict:** without Harvest, an Undead realm that finds no barrow has no engine at all. The one bright moment: the king walked into Divine's empty consecrated Bloodscar and every living company in his column rose a tier (the altar promotion is still 100% for the Undead; round 24 only limited Evil's).

**Evil (two realms).** Evil 3 is the kit working: a Titan province chained on turn 6, a Dark Pact every other turn (10.5g and 8 HP each), Terror on every neighbour, and an immediate war that walked into four empty Pagan provinces, the capital included. Then the Pagan storm (8g) killed 2–3 companies a cast and the Pagan king's Elite guard retook everything. Evil 9 is the kit failing: its first assault went into a Druidic jungle province (Forest Wardens silenced the archers, Home of the Wild ×1.5) and lost 5 of 7; it never had gold again, its beasts disbanded for wages, and the Druids took all four provinces. **Round-24 altar change** never applied: no Evil realm reached consecrated land.

**Divine.** The best-run kit this game. Seven consecrations (Tithes, Veteran levies), a Crusade against the wild that took Blightmire where the Undead failed three times, and a Proclaimed Crusade (×1.75 vs Undead) that took blighted Bloodscar and consecrated it on the spot. **Round-24 Divine Power:** this map put three holy sites inside Divine's land, so Power reached 10 by turn 14 (3 sites, 3 shining, Crusade 1, 7 consecrations = 3) and all six gifts were on; the consecration points alone would have given 3. None of the gifts did anything I could see: no Divine hero died, nobody cursed or charmed, no Terror reached Divine land. Weakness as before: the host left Bloodscar empty and the Undead walked in.

**Good (two realms).** Both rich (306g and 454g unspent): the Villages on tax ≤5 paid on most early provinces. **Round-24 Beacons:** Good 8 lit them twice (6 Veterans, 12g each time), they cost no wages, and each muster went home after three turns with a clear line per province. They never fought, because nobody attacked a Good province: the Good civil war was fought by walk-ins into empty provinces and two king-on-king battles (Good 2 lost 5 of 6 attacking Good 8 at Khazflats; Good 8 lost 2 of 5 attacking back). Hearts of Oak saved two heroes from lairs. The Good realms' problem was not money but having nothing to spend it on.

**Neutral (two realms).** Neutral 7 led the game by hiring bands at 1g, buying nothing it did not need and fighting only neutrals: 10 provinces, 495g. Neutral 1 bought Aldreach (40g, 4 defenders) and Highfell (29g, 3) by Trade Pact and ran two Embargoes on Good 2 (−20% tax), then lost Titandown to a walk-in. Its Sage reached 9 and drowned it in research choices (four or five a turn, most chosen by the scholars because none was sent). **Round-24 changes:** the pact was refused three times: on a creature province, on a Good province where Good's hero stood, and on a province of the realm the League was at war with (once peace came, the pact was still refused on the province it had just made peace over). Ransom was set ({"ransom":true}, challenge) on Valka all game; no personal encounter came, so it is still untested.

**Druidic.** Lost its king to a neutral guard's duel on turn 2 (the default "accept" stance), then won its war: Fangvale held against Evil 9 at 5 losses to 1, and the heir took Khazhold, Grimreach (the Evil capital), Fangexpanse and Anvilreach. Parley ×1.5 gave a free Ent at Fangvale. Wages kept it at 20–60g all game.

**Pagan.** The storm at 8g is still the Pagan's best order: 8 casts, 9 companies destroyed and about 10 demoted, two casts that destroyed nothing. It did not take provinces by itself any more; it softened the Evil king's host so the Pagan king could walk back in. Blood Offering raised two Dwarven Guard to Elite for two Average companies, and those two Elite carried every counterattack. Three sites woken (Favour ×1.24); the Old Stones never defended (the Evil king walked into empty stones). Pagan's weakness: one host, and the war took four provinces before it could answer.

## Report and order problems (new)

1. **A neutral garrison's single monster held a skeletal host three times** (Blightmire's Dread Hydras against 7 companies under the king at ×1.5): "Even without a commander, the garrison throws King0 back". The view showed "1 neutral defender", which reads as easy.
2. **The Undead's altar promotion still promotes every company at 100%** while Evil's now promotes one; worth stating in the kit text, since both read "casts down the altars".
3. **Divine Power is now shown only by the harness view line**; the realm's own report still never states Power or which gifts are on.
4. **A recruit order of two companies of one type gives one** ("already recruited this turn" for the second, at the hero's own province) and a second hero cannot recruit the same type there that turn.
5. **The Trade Pact refusal text lists every condition** and never says which one applied (three different refusals, one message).
6. **Proclaim Crusade the turn war is declared** says "none answers"; the war line in the same report says war "stands" from next turn. One line could join the two.
7. **Research at Sage 8–9 gives four or five choices a turn**; unanswered ones are chosen by the scholars, so most of Neutral 1's twenty-odd discoveries were never chosen by the player.
8. **A king's default "accept" killed the Druidic king on turn 2** against a neutral guard; the heir needed the kingSkills payload.

## Three balance observations

1. **The Undead need a reliable way to raise the dead.** Without Harvest and without a barrow in reach, King0's realm had 4–5 provinces all game and 0g at the end. Suggest counting the barrow and signature bonuses toward the Skeletal Armies cast chance (so trained Necromancy 2 casts at 70%, not 50%), and placing one barrow within two hexes of every Undead capital, as stones are placed beside every Pagan.
2. **Empty provinces decide the wars, not the kits.** Evil 3 took four Pagan provinces, Good 8 two of Good 2's, Good 2 one of Neutral 1's, and the Undead one consecrated Divine province, all by walking into provinces with no garrison. Suggest a free 1g watch company in every province a realm has held for three turns, or a warning line when an enemy host stands next to an empty province.
3. **Divine Power from consecrations works, but the gifts have nothing to do on most maps.** Power 10 and all six gifts changed nothing in this game. Suggest folding Sanctuary, Revelation and Absolution into one gift at Power 2, and giving the higher levels something that acts every turn (for example, Tithes +25% more at Power 4, or a free Veteran levy in each consecrated province at Power 6).
