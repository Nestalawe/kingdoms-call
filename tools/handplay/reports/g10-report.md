## Each alignment

**Undead.** What mattered: barrows (three held by turn 7, three graves each a turn), Skeletal Armies, Bind the Bones to Crack, fear ×1.5→×1.6, the Host-Leader cutting 15–17g of wages, and two blights paying grave goods. What did not: the king's spell. **Round-24 change:** without Harvest the Undead opening is a dice game: King0 (Necromancy 1 trained) fizzled seven casts in a row at 40–50% through turn 4, and his first skeletons came on turn 5. The cast chance uses the *trained* level only ("Necromancy 5 — 2 trained, +3 from items: 50%"), so the barrows' +1s and the signature +1 raise the companies per cast but not the chance. A second necromancer (Sombra, trained 4–5) fixed it from turn 7: 70–80%, three a cast. From turn 8 the host was 15–16 companies, mostly Crack, and it beat Neutral4's king 15 v 5 and took six provinces in six turns without a loss. Bind to Crack held: no skeletal company reached Elite except by battle. Verdict: the loop is slower (first real host turn 7, not turn 4) and still the strongest army on the board by turn 10.

**Good (both).** The Villages on tax ≤5 paid from turn 3 on every realm (Ironmarch 3, Moonvale 5, Lornroot 3, Highdelve 4, Urukrot 4): Good7 was the richest realm from turn 6 to the end (444g unspent). Shield of the Realm never fired (nobody attacked a Good province). **Round-24 Beacons:** lit four times (3–6 Veterans at 2g each, wage-free); the musters went home on schedule with a line each ("the muster of Stonevale is done… goes home"); no wage bill, no trap, but also no use: both Good realms were never attacked. Good1's war on Evil was a walk: the Evil capital was empty (walk-in, turn 11), then Elderreach and Beasthold against a king with 1–4 companies. Good Works found Terror only where capture had already wiped it. Hearts of Oak saved Briar from an Acid Wyvern.

**Pagan (both).** The engine again: Rite of Awakening at every stones province (Pagan2 woke five; Favour ×1.5 by turn 10), Blood Harvest paying 2g a kill and a promotion nearly every battle (King8's Shieldwall Levies were Elite by turn 5), the Wild Hunt binding two Veteran beasts for 4–5g (used three times; refused once: "needs a province where Creatures or Monsters still roam"), the Ley walked twice, Blood Offering giving two tiers (Average → Crack). **Round-24 storm at 8g:** cast three times: 0 destroyed + 1 demoted at Talondeep, 2 of 3 destroyed at Stormdelve, then the province fell to a walk-in. At 8g it still decided one province; the price made me cast it three times, not six. Pagan8 took the Divine capital on turn 14 with Favour ×1.5 and held it twice against a Proclaimed Crusade. Pagan weak spot: nothing; Pagan2 and Pagan8 were top three.

**Divine.** Consecrate + Tithes: +12g a turn by turn 13 on five consecrations; Blessed levies mustered Veterans; Crusade ×1.5 against beasts took Stormspire and Talonwood cheaply. **Round-24 Divine Power from consecrations:** five consecrated = Power 2 (Sanctuary, Grace abounding) with no holy site held, up from 0 in every earlier game; nothing in the report says the realm's Power or that a gift is on, and Power 3–6 were still out of reach in 16 turns. Proclaim Crusade ×1.5 lost twice on the Pagan-held capital (8 v 7, then 9 v 7: the capital's ×1.5 and Favour ×1.5 beat it). Divine's real failure was mine as its player: the capital stood empty while the king campaigned, and the Pagan king walked in.

**Neutral (both).** Neutral9 bought six provinces (Stormmarch 14g, Aerwood 15g, Goldmoor 44g, Redwold 48g and two more), each with its garrison standing, and took eight more from neutral defenders with four Veteran Forest Wardens; it never met an enemy in force and ended with the most land. Neutral4 bought three cheap provinces, lost its only lair-hunter to a Rock Giant, and was eaten by the Undead host in six turns: four companies against fifteen. **Round-24 Trade Pact:** the refusal fired twice, both on creature provinces (Talondrift, Wyrmdelve), with the new long message; no pact was tried on a province with an enemy hero or on an enemy realm's land. Embargo (5g) took 20% off the Undead for two turns (−12g in all). Ransom never came up: no Neutral hero ever fought a duel. Mercenary bands were hired for 1–3g throughout (the League hires best), and the pact's defenders stood as garrisons.

**Evil.** The best opening of the ten (Terror from Spread Dread, five provinces by turn 6, Dominion ×1.5 chaining Basilisks and Titans, Sable's skeletal beasts at Necromancy 5) and the worst ending: the war on the Druids broke on a jungle province and a mountain capital (13 companies attacking 6, eight lost), the Dark Pact cost 26.5g and 30 HP for three Average companies, monster wages failed three turns running (three beast garrisons disbanded), Good walked into the empty capital, and the king ended with one province and no army. **Round-24 altar promotion** never fired: Evil captured no consecrated province. Evil needs a garrison and a treasury before it needs a kit.

**Druidic.** Started worst (two repulses, 0g on turn 8, companies melting for wages) and ended as the realm that beat Evil: Home of the Wild ×1.5 at Starmire (jungle) threw back Savage Fury ×1.5, the mountain capital threw back 13 companies losing one, and on turn 13 five companies took Dreadwood (forest, Thunderbird province) from the Evil king. The wild breeds (bears, thunderbirds into garrisons) and Call of the Wild (2g a beast) worked; healers' fees were nil (no hero sat in a humanoid province). Grow the Wild never had a plains province to work on.

## Report and order problems (new)

1. **The Skeletal Armies cast chance ignores the barrow and signature bonuses** ("Necromancy 5 — 2 trained, +3 from items: 50%"). With Harvest gone this is the whole Undead opening; the text says the barrows add +1 Necromancy, and the player sees it do nothing for the roll.
2. **Nothing reports Divine Power.** Five consecrations gave Power 2 and no line, no "Sanctuary is yours" anywhere.
3. **A 'treat' offer by both sides in the same turn made an alliance** with no line in either report; the realms list simply said "allied" next turn. (It then blocked Good7's march into Pagan2's land: "turned back at the border… at peace", correct but a surprise.)
4. **Hero recruit orders cap silently at one company of a type per province per turn** ("already recruited this turn") and at the lead limit: the second Mammoth, the second Forest Wardens were refused with the reason only in the GM log.
5. **"cannot put to sea"** cost four king-phases: the view marks sea neighbours but a planned path two hexes out is blind.
6. **The storm's result line** ("0 companies are destroyed and 1 falls a quality tier") never names the companies hit; at 8g the player wants to know.
7. **Lair tiers kill heroes with Melee 5–6** (Brannoc, Melee 6, to a Rock Giant; Sythra, Melee 6, to a Hill Giant Chieftain) and the view shows no tier for those two kinds.
8. **A capital with no garrison falls to a walk-in** (Bonemoor, Easttor): the report says "seizes the undefended province", but nothing warns the owner the turn before that his capital is bare and an enemy stands next door.
9. **Good Works reports "no Terror or plague can touch the province"** even when there was none to clear; it never says whether it cleared anything.
10. **The Wild Hunt's beasts expire at the end of the following turn** whatever the march: both of Pagan2's hunts dissolved before a battle (one line: "slip the binding").

## Three balance observations

1. **The Undead without Harvest is a coin-toss opening, then the same wall.** Either let the barrow bonus count toward the cast chance (so a king at a held barrow casts at 60–70%, not 40%), or give the Undead king Necromancy 2 trained at the start; and keep Bind to Crack. The brake that matters is still barrow refill: three graves a turn at three barrows fed 16 companies by turn 8 once the casts landed.
2. **Capitals decide wars and nobody garrisons them.** Two capitals fell to walk-ins and one held 13 attackers with 6 defenders (×1.5 capital). Suggest a free 1g garrison company raised in every capital at the start, or a standing warning line "your capital has no garrison and an enemy host stands next door".
3. **Evil's monster wages are the kit's real enemy.** 7g a turn a Titan, 4g a Basilisk, 10.5g a Dark Pact; three times the realm could not pay and the beasts walked. Suggest monster companies at half wages while the realm holds the province that bred them, and the Dark Pact's company wage-free for three turns like the Wild Hunt's.
