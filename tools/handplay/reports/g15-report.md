**Pagan1 won** on land, tax and gold: six woken stones by turn 12, Favour at the ×1.5 cap from turn 6, two hosts, and three storms (8g each) on the Neutral capital that destroyed 8 of its 9 defenders before the assault. ## Each alignment

**Pagan (won, and fourth).** Rite of Awakening is the economy and the army in one: +3 tax a site and Favour to ×1.5 by the third site. Pagan1 had stones in six of its first nine provinces; Pagan8 in three of five. Call down the storm at 8g still decided the only siege of the game: three casts on a 9-company mountain garrison destroyed 8 companies and demoted 5 for 24g, and the capital fell to 6 attackers without loss. The Old Stones and Favour ×1.48 on home ground destroyed the Undead assault on Runemarch (turn 7) with no Pagan losses. Blood Harvest paid 2-8g a battle and promoted steadily (Pagan1's host was Elite by turn 11). Never used: Wild Hunt, Blood Offering, Ley (no need: the storm and Favour did everything). Round 24: 8g halved how often I stormed (three casts, not six) but not what a cast does; a cast still destroys a quarter of a garrison.

**Neutral (second and ninth).** Neutral7 bought six provinces for 11-26g each and ended with 14 provinces, the most land, and no army to speak of: every pact adds a garrison that draws full wages, and its wages ran at 44-49g against 52g of tax from turn 9. It never fought a player. Neutral0 bought three provinces and then lost the war it could not avoid: Trade Pacts, two Embargoes (−20% on Pagan1's 70g tax: 14g a turn, the best 5g in the game) and a 9-company capital garrison could not stop three storms. Round 24: the new pact refusals fired twice (a Pagan hero standing in Kronspire; Hornspire for a reason the message does not name). Ransom never arose: no Neutral duel.

**Good (third).** Isolated, never at war, the richest realm with 249g. The Villages paid on four provinces from turn 5. Round 24's Beacons: lit once on turn 13 for 12g, six Veterans into six garrisons, wages unchanged for three turns, and all six went home on turn 16 with a line each. That is what a muster should do; it would have been worth lighting only with an enemy next door. Shield of the Realm never fired. Good Works ran once and cleared nothing.

**Divine (fifth, and the best story).** Three provinces and 9g at turn 8 (two 2-defender plains repelled the king three times). Then the Druidic war: Proclaim Crusade (6g) gave ×1.5 and "the sword consecrates", so each of Marlstone (retaken), Goldstone, Lornroot, Thalroot, Magwells and Durvale was consecrated the turn it fell, with Tithes. Divine Power went from 2 to 6 on consecrations alone (8 consecrated, one holy site): the view lists all six gifts at the end, and Pilgrim roads appeared in the log; Sanctuary, Grace abounding, Revelation, Absolution and Church Triumphant were never reported as doing anything. Round 24's count works; the gifts themselves still need a line when they matter. Defend as a standing watch rode the king out of the capital to Redsands and won the war's decisive battle (5 of 6 Druidic companies destroyed, none lost).

**Undead (sixth, biggest army).** Without Harvest the Field the first six turns were slow: the king's Skeletal Armies at 50% raised one company from one grave; the two heroes who practised Necromancy from turn 1 reached 5 by turn 5 and 70-80%. The engine was the captured barrow of Myrcairn (turn 11): three graves a turn, three companies a cast at Necromancy 7, so 22 skeletal companies by turn 16 for about 40g of wages. Bind the Bones to Crack stopped the host at Crack: fine. The turn-7 assault on the Pagan capital failed because the two hosts fought separately (Nekros's 4 first, then the king's 6, each beaten by 7 defenders at ×1.48 and ×1.5). Blight twice (Aerglade, Grishexpanse): 2-3 free garrison skeletons a turn each. Lichdom, Desecrate, The soul is taken: not reached.

**Evil (seventh).** Terror and Spread Dread took the first three provinces cheaply, and then a 2-defender desert (Talonscar) repelled the king three times and a hero once, a 3-defender plain repelled him twice. With no monster province until turn 11 there was no Dominion, no Dark Pact, no Savage Fury; after Beastspire fell the Cave Trolls were chained, the pits bred, Dark Pact gave a company for 6g and 10 HP, and Talonscar finally fell. The altar promotion never arose (no consecrated province taken). Monster wages (7g each) left the realm at 10g.

**Druidic (eighth and last).** Druidic2 was repelled twice by the same 2-defender plain (3 of 5 lost each time), recruited past its income and ended at 0g shedding companies, though Parley ×1.5 and the Mammoths of Grimdown finally worked. Druidic9 walked into the empty Divine capital and then lost every battle to a 4-company Crusade host in its own forest (Forest Wardens silenced the Divine archers; it did not matter). Its king fled four times with his last company. Call of the Wild, Grow the Wild, the breeding never came into play.

## Report and order problems (new)

1. Two friendly hosts entering the same enemy province in the same turn fight separate battles, each against the full garrison. Nothing in the order form says so; a player will expect a combined assault.
2. The Trade Pact refusal is one message for five conditions; Hornspire was refused and I cannot tell which rule bit.
3. A successful Skeletal Armies roll at a province with no graves prints the success and raises nothing (Thornax, turn 5): the graves check should come before the roll, or the line should say the field was empty.
4. Divine Power's gifts are listed in the view but the log never says when Sanctuary, Grace abounding, Revelation, Absolution or Church Triumphant did anything.
5. A king on Defend in his capital rides out to a neighbouring province's defence (Redsands, turn 11). It won the war here, but the capital was empty for the phase.
6. The storm's line to its target says "1 destroyed in all" but never names the companies; the caster's realm gets no line at all.
7. A 2-defender neutral province in open terrain repelled 4-6 company hosts nine times in this game (Hornwold ×2, Ancientridge ×2, Talonscar ×4, Hornspire ×2). "2 neutral defenders" tells a player nothing about a Veteran Titan Warbeast pair.
8. Harness (my own, additive, none under `src/`): `cs15.py` (a compact state print with neighbours' defender counts), `t15.sh`/`run15.sh`/`stand15.py` copies of the g10 helpers; no engine changes.

## Three balance observations

1. **The storm at 8g is still the siege engine.** Three casts (24g) destroyed 8 of 9 defenders of a capital over three turns, with no counter and no line to the caster. Suggest: a province can be stormed once every two turns (the stones need rest), or the storm cannot destroy more than one company a cast.
2. **The Undead without Harvest is a barrow race.** Before a barrow: one company every other turn. After one: nine a turn's worth of graves and a 22-company host in five turns. Suggest: a captured barrow refills one grave a turn (three only at the capital's own barrow), and the kit guarantees a barrow within two hexes of the Undead capital, so the opening is not luck.
3. **Divine on Crusade snowballs harder than any kit now.** "The sword consecrates" plus Divine Power from consecrations took Divine from 3 provinces to 9 and Power 6 in six turns with four companies. Suggest: the sword consecrates only provinces taken from the Crusade's named realm while the Crusade lasts (it did), and consecrations count for Power only while a hero stands in or next to them, or cap the consecration bonus at +2.
