# R-003: Title intelligence reports on every rival realm

- **Decided:** 2026-09-17, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** GM portal comment "ALL THREE ARE NOW RIVAL-BY-RIVAL BOARDS"
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

The intelligence a title holder receives (the Cartographer, Beastlord and Wildwarden included) gives one line per rival realm, opening with the holder's own figure, the same shape as the Throne, Vaultkeeper, Grand Marshal and Sea Lord. Realms the holder has not met are left out ([R-046](R-046-unmet-realms-are-nameless.md)).

In Toby's words: “each should say something about every OTHER player.”
