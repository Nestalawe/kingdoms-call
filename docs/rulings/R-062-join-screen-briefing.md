# R-062: The join-screen briefings stay short and current

- **Decided:** 2026-09-27, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.27-1719 §256 ("STANDING RULE (Toby, 2026-09-27)")
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

The race, alignment and temper boxes on the join screen are what most players read before committing to a realm for a whole game. They stay short (what a newcomer needs to choose) and are updated in the same change as any rule they describe: fervour rates, signature orders, racial bonuses, temper figures. The detail belongs in the rulebook.
