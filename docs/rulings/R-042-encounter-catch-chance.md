# R-042: Catching a quarry with Encounter Character

- **Decided:** 2026-09-24, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.23-2234 §57 (item 1)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

The chance to catch a quarry is 50% + 5% per Spy level of the hunter − 5% per Spy level of the quarry, between 5% and 100%, whether or not the quarry leads an army. (The camp-infiltration roll against an army leader is separate.)
