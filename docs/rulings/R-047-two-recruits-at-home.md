# R-047: Two recruits a season only in your founding capital

- **Decided:** 2026-09-24, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.24-1319 §141 (item 10)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A realm may recruit two of a unit type per season only in its own founding capital, not in a captured one. Everywhere else the limit is one.

**Open:** Garrison units and fleets keep two per season anywhere: the agent read "except garrisons and naval units" as "those keep their own rule". Confirm, or say they should not get two even at home. (Goblin Hordes' two a turn is separate: [R-096](R-096-goblins-two-a-turn.md).)
