# R-041: Every modifier first, pre-battle damage last

- **Decided:** 2026-09-24, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.23-2234 §60 (build v2026.09.23-2234, Toby, with his ruling on spells)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A battle side's strength is worked out in order: units, then command, then whole-force modifiers; only then is pre-battle damage (volleys, battle magic, strikes, flak) taken off, never below 25% of that figure; then the battle lines. Terror is a modifier (×0.9 a stack), not damage. Psychic Blast, Curse and Telekinetic Storm still change a modifier, not strength.
