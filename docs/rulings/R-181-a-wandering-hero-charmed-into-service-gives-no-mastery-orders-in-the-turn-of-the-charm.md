# R-181: A wandering hero charmed into service gives no mastery orders in the turn of the charm

- **Decided:** 2026-11-15, a maintainer
- **Status:** Decided, not built
- **Source:** Question 49: may a wandering hero charmed into service be given his mastery orders and mastery spells in the turn of the charm? Answer: option 1, no: from the next turn he is an ordinary hero.
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

In the turn a wandering hero is charmed into your service, you don't know his levels yet, so he may be given no
mastery orders (Vanish, Grand Heist, …) and no mastery spells, as for a hero hired that turn
([R-048](R-048-heroes-not-yet-joined.md)). From the next turn he is an ordinary hero, and the rulebook says so ("from
the next turn"). Example: Mira the Grey-Eyed, a wandering hero with Thief 9, is charmed into Lord Thorn's service in
Spring: for the rest of that turn she can't be ordered to Vanish. Today the order form hides those orders, but the
turn accepts them, and the rulebook says a hero charmed into permanent service "keeps every order".

**Note:** Decided, not yet built. Left: the dispatch's check for a hero who joined this turn (the one #365 made for a
hired hero) covers a hero charmed this turn too, a unit case first; then the rulebook's line.

The question was: Mira the Grey-Eyed is charmed into your service in Spring; the form doesn't offer her mastery orders
that turn. Option 1: "no masteries in the turn she is charmed, as the form does today; from the next turn she is an
ordinary hero; the rulebook says 'from the next turn'".
