# R-053: Spiritwrack silences

- **Decided:** 2026-09-26, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.26-1536 §236 item 3
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

In personal combat Spiritwrack silences its target (no casting, no archery or powers, melee halved) for 1 + half the caster's level rounds, at most 4. Against a guardian weak to White Magic it takes at 95% and lasts one round longer (at most 5). From the second round, a Powerful guardian has a 25% chance each round to tear free, a Legendary one 40%; characters cannot.
