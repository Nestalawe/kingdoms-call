# R-153: The Ring of the Unbroken Pulse and the Great Helm of the Deathless shrug off a killing blow; four more items state the 30%

- **Decided:** 2026-10-31, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Question 25: six second-wind items don't say the 30%, and two describe another rule: which is right? Answer: option 2, the Ring and the Helm get the rule their texts give, and the four others' texts state the 30%.
- **Enforced by:** [`the-ring-and-the-helm-shrug-off-a-killing-blow-once-a-fight`](../../tests/unit/the-ring-and-the-helm-shrug-off-a-killing-blow-once-a-fight.test.ts), [`every-copy-of-the-ring-the-helm-and-the-second-wind-items-takes-its-rows-effect-and-words`](../../tests/unit/every-copy-of-the-ring-the-helm-and-the-second-wind-items-takes-its-rows-effect-and-words.test.ts)

The Ring of the Unbroken Pulse and the Great Helm of the Deathless do what their texts say: a chance to shrug off a
killing blow and stand at 1 HP, which can save the wearer once a fight. A failed roll doesn't spend it: he rolls at
each killing blow until it has saved him once. Only a blow is shrugged off: a burn, poison or bleeding that kills him
at a round's start is not (a maintainer's answers, 27 November). The other second-wind items keep their rule (the
first time the bearer falls below 30% of his hit points in a fight, he recovers a share of his maximum), and their
texts say the 30%. Example: a killing blow falls on Sir Aldric, who wears the Ring; on its chance he stands at 1 HP
instead.

Every copy already in a running game (a hero's items, a lair's hoard, a realm's vault) changes too: at the start of
each turn it takes its item's effect and words, so an older Ring or Helm loses its second wind, and the four other
items say the 30% (question 166, option 2: a maintainer's answer, 27 November).

The question was: six second-wind items don't say the 30%, and two describe another rule. Option 2: "the Ring and the
Helm get the rule their texts give, and the four others' texts state the 30%".

In the maintainer's words: "(2)"
