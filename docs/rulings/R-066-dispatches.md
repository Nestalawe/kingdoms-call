# R-066: Dispatches between players

- **Decided:** 2026-09-27, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.27-2047 §291–297
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Players send dispatches to any realm they have met, to any combination of them, or publicly to everyone in the game. Dispatches arrive at once, not on the turn cycle, and stay between turns. They cannot be edited or withdrawn. They are absent from a game with fewer than two human realms, and bots cannot be addressed. Who reads private dispatches, and the limits: [R-073](R-073-private-dispatches.md), [R-074](R-074-dispatch-limits.md).
