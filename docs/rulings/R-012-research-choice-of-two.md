# R-012: Research offers a choice of two discoveries

- **Decided:** 2026-09-21, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.21-1459 §4
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A successful ordinary Research roll draws two discoveries from the field. The player keeps one when sending the next orders; it takes effect from that turn. With no choice sent, one is picked at random. The other is forgotten for good. The hidden-field side-find and Research Breakthrough stay single finds.
