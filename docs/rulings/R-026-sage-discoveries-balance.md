# R-026: Sage discoveries: each one unique, none weak or strong

- **Decided:** 2026-09-22, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.22-0706 (Toby's brief and mid-session decisions)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

There are 296 Sage discoveries, and no two share an effect anywhere in the table or within any realm's research pool. Each is tuned to be worth about 1.5 gold a turn to an engaged player (1–2), with care that Economy discoveries do not unbalance income or Military ones make armies too strong. Stacked discovery strength on a unit is capped at ×1.30. Existing discoveries may be re-scoped; a weak mechanic gets a second, smaller effect.

**Open:** Most income discoveries pay "+1g a turn". Is that the feel Toby wants? Lowering the gold weighting lifts most to +2g.
