# R-021: Only a king may run down another king

- **Decided:** 2026-09-21, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.21-1033 §6 (item 6)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A king may hunt down another king with Encounter Character, and only a king may. Between kings it is always a duel. The mutual-challenge exception applies to commoners only.
