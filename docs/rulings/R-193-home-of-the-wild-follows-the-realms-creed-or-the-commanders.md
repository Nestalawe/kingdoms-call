# R-193: Home of the Wild follows the realm's creed or the commander's

- **Decided:** 2026-11-28, a maintainer
- **Status:** Decided, not built
- **Source:** Question 79 (2026-11-14): does a Druidic hero bring Home of the Wild to another realm's Creatures? Answer: option 1, as played: the realm's creed or the commander's, and the books say so; the maintainers' answer of 28 November.
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Home of the Wild gives Creature companies ×1.5 in forest and jungle when their realm is Druidic, or when the hero who
leads them is. Example: Sir Bram, a Druidic hero hired by a Good realm, leads Great Bears in the Elder Wood; they fight
×1.5, as they would under a Druidic king. A garrison with no hero has only its realm's creed
([R-164](R-164-a-garrison-with-no-hero-fights-with-its-realms-rules.md)). The battle does this today.

**Note:** Decided, not yet built: the words. The rulebook, the intro, the report and the join form say only "your
Creature armies" of a Druidic realm; they are to say that a Druidic hero brings it too.

In the maintainers' words: "(1)"
