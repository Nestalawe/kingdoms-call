# R-044: Breaking an alliance makes realms neutral

- **Decided:** 2026-09-24, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.24-1319 §138 (item 6)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A war declaration against an ally breaks the alliance and leaves the two realms Neutral. An Evil or Undead crown that breaks an alliance goes straight to war. The Diplomacy card says Break Alliance (Break Alliance — War for an Evil or Undead viewer).
