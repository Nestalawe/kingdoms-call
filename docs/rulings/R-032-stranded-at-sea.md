# R-032: Stranded at sea

- **Decided:** 2026-09-23, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.23-1934 §28 and §34 (Toby's rulings on the review list)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A column on open water must stay carried by its own ships; ships are never shared between characters. When it cannot be carried, the weakest land units go ashore: to an adjacent province the realm owns, else into the sea province's garrison if the realm owns it, else the sea is claimed if unowned and undefended; in another realm's or defended water they are lost. A beaten landing may fall back to its ships. A force that cannot be carried may retreat onto friendly shore but cannot sail on. A commander dying on unowned, undefended water claims it for his garrison. Unpaid wages never disband a ship its column needs, and let it go once it is not needed. Disband Army at sea is allowed; Pick Up at sea loads in pick order.
