# R-018: What every new world guarantees

- **Decided:** 2026-09-21, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.21-1121 §2 (Toby's setup rules, audited); v2026.09.21-1611 §6
- **Enforced by:** [`home-placement`](../../tests/home-placement.test.js)

Every home province is tax 10, owned, explored and of the king's race, with at least 2 land neighbours, at most 2 sea and at most 1 primal; among its neighbours one Poor and one Prosperous humanoid province, one of the king's own race. Every home holds a Weak lair and never a Legendary one. A naval king can build a fleet at home; a coastal home touches undefended open water; a Pagan king borders a ritual site; a Druidic king borders a Creature province (an Evil king a Monster province: [R-104](R-104-evil-and-druidic-crowns.md)). Capitals are at least 4 hexes apart and never on the top or bottom row ([R-057](R-057-capitals-far-apart.md)). Each king starts at 15 HP (18 Orcish) with Melee 2, Archery 1, Sage 1 and two land armies (Undead: one more, skeletal); each realm has two heroes with 3 skill points, the first of the king's race, the second never of a feuding race. Every world has exactly 22 Legendary lairs, neutral races within one province of each other, and neutral garrisons sized by the tax band.
