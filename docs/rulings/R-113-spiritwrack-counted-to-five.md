# R-113: Spiritwrack counts White to 5

- **Decided:** 2026-10-15, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** PR #89 item 7 (decision D5 on a player's rulebook review)
- **Enforced by:** [`spiritwrack-five`](../../tests/spiritwrack-five.test.js)

Replaces R-053's limits. In personal combat Spiritwrack silences its target (no casting, no archery or powers,
melee halved) for 1 round plus 1 for every two levels of White Magic, with the level counted to 5 like every
other spell's effect: 1 round at White 1, 2 at White 2–3, 3 at White 4 and up. Against a guardian weak to White
Magic it takes hold at 95% and lasts one round longer, so at most 4. The rest of R-053 stands: from the second
round a Powerful guardian has a 25% chance each round to tear free, a Legendary one 40%; characters cannot.

The game already played this way, because a character's school level in a duel was already counted to 5; the
books promised 4 rounds (5 against a weak guardian), which no caster could reach.
