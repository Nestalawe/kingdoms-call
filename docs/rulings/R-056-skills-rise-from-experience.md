# R-056: How skills rise from experience

- **Decided:** 2026-09-27, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.26-1536 §236 item 7, v2026.09.27-1719 §250 and §253 (item 5), v2026.09.29-1452 §391
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A character rises at most one skill per phase: every skill that earns a rise that phase is a candidate, and one is drawn at random at the end of the phase. A skill may rise from experience only once a turn; Practice is outside that rule (exactly as Toby asked) and a Practice success takes the phase. In a fight, every landed hit or successful cast rolls for experience, and the first success ends the rolls for that fighter in that fight; the loser rolls too.

In Toby's words: “They should roll per success, with no more rolls after the first successful one.”
