# R-161: A commander slain in the duel before a battle takes his skills out of it

- **Decided:** 2026-11-15, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Question 52: the two kinds of battle count the skills of a commander slain in the duel otherwise: which is right? Answer: option 1, his skills leave the battle in both: no Druid, no Archery. Built in P1-23am (#843). Question 162 (2026-11-27): does a commander whose skill is cut (cursed, blasted, or slain in the duel) keep his mastery tiers' gifts? Answer: option 1, they stay, as built; a maintainer's answer, 27 November.
- **Enforced by:** [`a-commander-slain-in-the-duel-before-a-battle-takes-his-skills-out-of-it`](../../tests/unit/a-commander-slain-in-the-duel-before-a-battle-takes-his-skills-out-of-it.test.ts)

A commander who falls in the duel before a battle takes his skills out of it, in both kinds of battle: his
companies fight without his Druid, and his Archery adds nothing to his bowmen's volley. Example: Sir Aldric (Druid 4)
leads Cave Trolls against a province's guards and falls in the duel; his trolls fight short of the Druid they need, as
they would against Lady Wren's host. His Yeoman Longbows loose their volley before the battle with no Archery behind
it, in both kinds of battle.

A commander slain in the duel keeps his mastery tiers' gifts: Druid 8's lift of his beasts' quality, Necromancy 6's,
Tactical 8's spared host and Naval 8's sea waiver read his trained level.

The question was: the two battles count a slain commander's skills otherwise. Option 1: "a slain commander's skills
leave the battle in both: no Druid, no Archery".

In the maintainer's words: "(1)"
