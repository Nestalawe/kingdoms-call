# R-161: A commander slain in the duel before a battle takes his skills out of it

- **Decided:** 2026-11-15, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Plan question 52 (Toby's answer of 2026-11-15); not yet built
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A commander who falls in the duel before a battle takes his skills out of it, in both kinds of battle: his
companies fight without his Druid, and his Archery adds nothing to his bowmen's volley. Example: Sir Aldric (Druid 4)
leads Cave Trolls against a province's guards and falls in the duel; his trolls fight without his Druid, as they would
against Lady Wren. Today, against guards, they keep his Druid; and between heroes his Archery still adds to the volley.
