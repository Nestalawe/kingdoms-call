# R-132: Four more creed orders' notes follow the turn

- **Decided:** 2026-11-05, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Plan question 41, built in PR #PRNUM (parts a and b)
- **Enforced by:** [`the-player-portals-copies-agree-with-the-engine`](../../tests/unit/the-player-portals-copies-agree-with-the-engine.test.ts)

As [R-131](R-131-the-creed-orders-notes-follow-the-turn.md): each creed order's note says what the turn will do.

Part (a), Consecrate's home: the form's home is the turn's, from the report: the capital the realm was founded on,
held or lost. Example: Queen Isolde holds her own Highmere and a captured Ashfall (tax 10); her note no longer calls
Ashfall her home. If Highmere is taken, the provinces beside it are still her home marches, as the turn counts them.

Part (b), Consecrate's limit: the king's White counts to 9, as the turn counts it. Example: King Aldric has White 7;
his note's limit is 7, not 5.

The form gives the turn's six checks in the turn's order (his realm's province; not the home marches; not consecrated
already; beside no consecrated province; he knows White; fewer than he may hold). A report written before the home and
the limit were in it knows no home and reads his trained White, for one turn at most.

Parts (c) and (d) are built in a later row and pinned until then: Harvest the Field's price with Corpse-Carters, and
the Wild Hunt's term with The Long Hunt.

The question was: four notes said otherwise than the turn. Option 1: "each note follows the turn". Option 2 (not
taken): the turn follows the notes.
