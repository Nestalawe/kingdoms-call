- **Status:** Confirmed

A realm's Steel Armour and Military Discipline speak of its own companies, in any battle. Against a province's
neutral guards, Steel Armour spares the realm's own companies only (the beaten guards die at the plain chance), and a
beaten host keeps Military Discipline's better chance to be promoted. Example: Sir Aldric's realm holds both. Beaten by
a province's guards, his companies keep Military Discipline, as they would against Lady Wren. Today, against guards,
Steel Armour spares the beaten guards too, and a beaten host loses Military Discipline.
