# R-160: Steel Armour and Military Discipline work against neutral guards as between heroes

- **Decided:** 2026-11-15, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Question 51: the battle against neutral guards reads Steel Armour and Military Discipline otherwise than the battle between heroes: which is right? Answer: option 1, the battle against guards follows the battle between heroes.
- **Enforced by:** [`steel-armour-and-military-discipline-read-a-sides-own-companies-against-neutral-guards-as-between-heroes`](../../tests/unit/steel-armour-and-military-discipline-read-a-sides-own-companies-against-neutral-guards-as-between-heroes.test.ts)

A realm's Steel Armour and Military Discipline speak of its own companies, in any battle. Against a province's
neutral guards, Steel Armour spares the realm's own companies only (the beaten guards die at the plain chance), and a
beaten host keeps Military Discipline's better chance to be promoted. Example: Sir Aldric's realm holds both. Beaten by
a province's guards, his companies keep Military Discipline, as they would against Lady Wren.

The question was: the battle against guards read the two discoveries otherwise. Option 1: "the battle against guards
follows the battle between heroes".

In the maintainer's words: "(1)"
