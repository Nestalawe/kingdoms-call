Matchups run ×0.75 to ×1.25 by unit type. A wider front wraps the enemy's ends (×1.2) and the wrapped end is struck from the side (×0.9). Spare flank riders guard the ends. An unopposed flank rider fights at ×1.3 and turns the flank (second-line ranged ×0.75). A line anchored by infantry at both ends cannot be turned.
