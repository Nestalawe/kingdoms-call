# R-149: The lancer's, the legion's and the bowman's items name every unit the battle lifts

- **Decided:** 2026-10-31, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Question 21: the lancer's, the legion's and the bowman's items name fewer units than the battle lifts: which is right? Answer: option 1, the texts say what the battle does. Question 175 (asked in #863 and #864): does KD-029's Decision 3 cover this row's re-stamp? Answer: yes, every copy in play is re-stamped; the maintainers' answer of 28 November.
- **Enforced by:** [`the-commanders-items-texts-name-every-unit-the-battle-lifts`](../../tests/unit/the-commanders-items-texts-name-every-unit-the-battle-lifts.test.ts)

The items that lift one kind of company in battle say every unit the battle lifts, and the siege items say what
the battle does. Example: the Golden Spur lifts Sir Aldric's Warg Riders, Eagle Riders, Noble Griffons, Stag Riders and
Ironhoof Rams as well as his lances, and its text names them all. The Legionnaire's Mark and the Eagle-Eye Pendant
name their units the same way, and the siege rings and cloaks say the defenders' walls are halved, as the Siege
Master's Ring does. Nothing changes in play. A copy already in a running game takes its row's texts at the turn's
start.

The question was: the items' texts name fewer units than the battle gives. Option 1: "the texts say what the battle
does".

In the maintainer's words: "(1)"
