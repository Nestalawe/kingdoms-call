# R-149: The lancer's, the legion's and the bowman's items name every unit the battle lifts

- **Decided:** 2026-10-31, a maintainer
- **Status:** Decided, not built
- **Source:** Question 21: the lancer's, the legion's and the bowman's items name fewer units than the battle lifts: which is right? Answer: option 1, the texts say what the battle does.
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

The items that lift one kind of company in battle say every unit the battle lifts, and the siege items say what
the battle does. Example: the Golden Spur lifts Sir Aldric's Warg Riders, Eagle Riders, Noble Griffons, Stag Riders and
Ironhoof Rams as well as his lances, and its text names them all. The Legionnaire's Mark and the Eagle-Eye Pendant
name their units the same way, and the siege rings and cloaks say the defenders' walls are halved, as the Siege
Master's Ring does. Nothing changes in play; today about thirty texts name fewer units, or another rule.
