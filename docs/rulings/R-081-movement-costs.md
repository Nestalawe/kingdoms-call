# R-081: Movement costs of conquest

- **Decided:** 2026-10-08, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** PR #8, ruling 6
- **Enforced by:** [`move-points`](../../tests/move-points.test.js)

Taking an undefended enemy province costs 1 movement point; pressing on with a battle against neutral defenders costs 1 a phase while points remain; winning costs an extra point from the right pool. A lone hero (no army) cannot claim land.
