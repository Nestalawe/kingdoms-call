# R-034: Tactical lifts flyers as it lifts land units

- **Decided:** 2026-09-23, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.23-1934 §31 (item 5)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A commander's Tactical applies to flyers exactly as to land units, in land battles only. Fleets gain nothing from Tactical.
