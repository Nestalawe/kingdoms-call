# R-072: Guardian hit points by grade

- **Decided:** 2026-09-29, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.29-2331 §412 (supersedes §241); benchmark ladder in the current-state summary
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Guardian HP bands: Weak 11–17, Moderate 19–25, Powerful 32–42, Legendary 53–65 (Moderate and above about 75% of the earlier bands, every band narrowed, each guardian keeping its rank within its grade). Flat heals were scaled with their guardian. The ladder is meant for a hero who has outgrown the grade below: Weak about 15 HP, no items, Melee 3 / Archery 2; Moderate about 30 HP with first-rung kit; Powerful about 45 HP with second-rung kit; Legendary about 60 HP with third-rung kit.

In Toby's words: “C4's Moderate cut to 75%, Powerful and Legendary cut to 75% too, and every band narrowed.”
