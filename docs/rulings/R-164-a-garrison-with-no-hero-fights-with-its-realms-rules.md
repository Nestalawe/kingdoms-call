# R-164: A realm's garrison with no hero fights with its realm's rules, the new alignment abilities included

- **Decided:** 2026-11-15, the maintainers (questions 55, 78 and 80)
- **Status:** Decided, not built
- **Source:** Questions 55, 78 and 80 (2026-11-15): does a garrison with no hero fight with its realm's rules, does such a garrison on consecrated ground roll zeal, and does the answer hold for the new alignment abilities too? Answer: option 1 to the first and the last: it fights with its realm's rules, the new abilities included; so it rolls its creed's zeal, at double the chance on consecrated ground.
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A realm's garrison that fights with no hero fights with its realm's rules, as a hero's host would: Sanctuary's
cut to the attacker, the realm's creed (its fanaticism, Home of the Wild, Raiders' Muster on a win), Steel Armour and
promotion for a winning garrison, the siege cuts against its home ground's ×1.2, and the new alignment abilities once
they are built. The battle runs as against a hero's host, not as against neutral guards. Example: Sir Aldric attacks
Lady Wren's town, held by her garrison alone; it fights with every rule of her realm. Today such a battle runs as against neutral guards.

**Zeal:** its fanaticism included, such a garrison rolls its creed's zeal, at double the chance on consecrated
ground, as the books say. Example: Brother Osric's garrison of Holy Spearmen, alone in a consecrated province of a
Divine realm, rolls zeal at double its creed's chance. Today a garrison alone never rolls zeal.

**Note:** Decided, not yet built.

The questions were: question 55, a garrison with no hero fights without its realm's rules (option 1: "a garrison
fights with its realm's rules, as a hero's host does"); question 78, whether a garrison on consecrated ground rolls zeal (answered by 55: it
fights with its realm's creed, its fanaticism included); question 80, whether 55 holds for the new alignment abilities too (option 1: "55 wins").

In the maintainers' words: "(1)" (question 55); "(1), 55 wins" (question 80)
