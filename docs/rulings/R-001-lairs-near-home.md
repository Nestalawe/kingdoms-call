# R-001: Lairs near home: 60% Weak, 30% Moderate, 10% Powerful

- **Decided:** 2026-09-14, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** GM portal comment above lair grading ("This is Toby's rule"); DEV_LOG current-state summary
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

In the home band (a home province and every province next to one), lairs are exactly 60% Weak, 30% Moderate and 10% Powerful, drawn by lot, on every map. Further out, grades shade by distance (with deliberate jitter) so there is variety at every distance. No Legendary lair sits in a home province.
