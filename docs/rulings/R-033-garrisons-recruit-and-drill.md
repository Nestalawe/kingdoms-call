# R-033: Garrison troops muster into the garrison and drill themselves

- **Decided:** 2026-09-23, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.23-1934 §29–30 (items 3–4)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Garrison unit types recruited in a province you own go straight into its garrison, whatever "Muster to" says. Each turn every unled garrison unit in an owned province may improve as if drilled at Tactical 1 (fleets and sea-beasts at Naval 1); skeletal, temporary, phantom, Wild Hunt and Elite units excepted.
