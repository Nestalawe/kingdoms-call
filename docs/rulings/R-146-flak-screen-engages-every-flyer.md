# R-146: Flak Screen engages every flyer, beasts too

- **Decided:** 2026-10-31, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Question 18: does Flak Screen engage every enemy flyer, as its blurb says, or winged riders only, as the turn and the rulebook do? Answer: option 2, every flyer, beasts too, as the blurb reads. Built in P1-23n.
- **Enforced by:** [`flak-screen-engages-every-flyer-winged-riders-and-flying-beasts-in-both-kinds-of-battle`](../../tests/unit/flak-screen-engages-every-flyer-winged-riders-and-flying-beasts-in-both-kinds-of-battle.test.ts)

Each Gnomish Gyrocopter's Flak Screen engages one enemy flying unit before the battle (−30% strength), as its
blurb says: winged riders and flying beasts alike (Crimson Rocs, Thunderbirds, Elder Dragons, Manticores). Example:
Lady Wren's gyrocopters meet Varn's Wyvern Riders and Crimson Rocs; either may be engaged.

The question was: the blurb says one enemy flying unit; the turn and the rulebook a winged rider. Option 2: "Flak
Screen engages every flyer, beasts too, as the blurb reads".

In the maintainer's words: "(2)"
