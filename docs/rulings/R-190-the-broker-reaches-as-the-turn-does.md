# R-190: The Broker reaches as the turn does

- **Decided:** 2026-11-24, a maintainer
- **Status:** Decided, not built
- **Source:** A maintainer's answer of 2026-11-24, in the scoping of Run 3 of T7 (B-06): the Broker keeps the reach the turn gives him today, and the words follow the turn. It supersedes [R-156](R-156-the-broker-reaches-what-the-realms-map-shows.md), the answer to question 35.
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A Neutral king's Broker reaches as the turn has him reach. He hires a wandering hero who stands in a province his
realm holds or has visited, and he buys word, at its price in the Broker's row, from a province his realm holds, has
visited or has explored; the rulebook and the creed text say so. Example: King Aldric of a Neutral realm took
Brackenford last season. A wandering ranger stands there now: Aldric's Broker can hire him, wherever the king stands,
at the ranger's hire wage. Brackenford's neighbour Fallowmere, never entered nor explored, is out of the Broker's
reach for word until his realm has visited or explored it.

**Note:** Decided, not yet built: the turn already does this; B-20 makes the words follow it.
