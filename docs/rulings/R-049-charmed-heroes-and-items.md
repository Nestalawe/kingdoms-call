# R-049: A charmed hero handles no items

- **Decided:** 2026-09-24, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.24-1554 §160
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A charmed hero may not be given an item, may not use one, and may not send one to anybody. He keeps his own gear, fights with it, and its passive magic still works. (The caster still learns the charmed hero's skills and items when the Charm lands.)

**Supersedes:** The 2026-09-12 and 2026-09-18 rulings on charmed heroes sending items.
