# R-040: Raid the Coast and Reaver Crews in a landing

- **Decided:** 2026-09-23, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.23-2139 §49–50, v2026.09.23-2153 §54
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Raid the Coast's battle bonus needs the raid order, a landing from the sea this turn, and the same province: marching overland to a coast does not count. When the raider lands with allies and one of them takes command, the raid (like Reaver Crews' amphibious assault) carries over to whoever commands the pooled landing.
