# R-159: A Curse in battle halves a master Druid's whole Druid

- **Decided:** 2026-11-15, a maintainer
- **Status:** Decided, not built
- **Source:** Question 50: the battle's Druid check and the Druid mastery halve a cursed Druid differently: which is right? Answer: option 1, the check halves his whole Druid, as the mastery does.
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A Curse cast on a commander during a battle halves his whole Druid in the battle's Druid check, as his Druid
mastery does: the check doesn't first stop his Druid at 5. One Druid level counts in every rule
([R-121](R-121-a-curse-spell-reaches-a-commanders-druid-in-battle.md)). Example: Sir Aldric (Druid 8) leads Titan
Warbeasts, which need Druid 5. Varn's mage curses him in the battle: his Druid is 4, one level short, so his Titans
fight at 80%. Today the check stops him at 5 and halves that to 2, and they fight at 40%.

**Note:** Decided, not yet built.

The question was: the Druid check and the mastery halve a cursed Druid differently. Option 1: "the check halves his
whole Druid, as the mastery does".
