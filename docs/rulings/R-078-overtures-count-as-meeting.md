# R-078: An overture introduces the sender

- **Decided:** 2026-10-08, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** PR #8, ruling 3
- **Enforced by:** [`overtures`](../../tests/overtures.test.js)

Being offered peace or alliance counts as meeting the sender, and incoming offers show on the Diplomacy card. In a game of three realms or fewer, an impossible alliance offer is not sent.
