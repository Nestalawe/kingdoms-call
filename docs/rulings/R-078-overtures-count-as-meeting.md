# R-078: An overture introduces the sender

- **Decided:** 2026-10-08, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** PR #8, ruling 3
- **Enforced by:** [`an-overture-from-a-stranger-counts-as-meeting-and-no-impossible-alliance-is-offered`](../../tests/scenario/an-overture-from-a-stranger-counts-as-meeting-and-no-impossible-alliance-is-offered.test.ts), [`the-player-portals-diplomacy-card-shows-the-offer-a-realm-received`](../../tests/page/the-player-portals-diplomacy-card-shows-the-offer-a-realm-received.test.ts)

Being offered peace or alliance counts as meeting the sender, and incoming offers show on the Diplomacy card. In a game of three realms or fewer, an impossible alliance offer is not sent.
