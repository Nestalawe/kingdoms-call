# R-007: When a Defending general rides out

- **Decided:** 2026-09-19, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG current-state summary ("THE DEFEND STEP (v2026.09.19-1644, Toby's ruling)"); changelog v2026.09.19-1644
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A general on Defend rides out only after every non-moving order of the phase (recruits, hand-overs, spells, items) and before any Move. So orders aimed at him that phase reach him at home, and a host raised or summoned in his watch must beat him as the defender rather than taking empty ground.
