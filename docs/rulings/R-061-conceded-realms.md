# R-061: A realm that has conceded

- **Decided:** 2026-09-27, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.27-1719 §252 (items 7–8)
- **Enforced by:** [`concede`](../../tests/concede.test.js)

A conceded or eliminated realm shows as such on the Diplomacy card, with no Treat or Declare controls. When every rival but one has conceded, the Reckoning is called at once: the last realm standing wins and every realm's Legacy is set down.
