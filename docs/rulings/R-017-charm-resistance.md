# R-017: Charm resistance grows 30% per Charm

- **Decided:** 2026-09-21, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.21-1121 items 3–5
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Each Charm that has ever taken hold on a character adds 30% to their resistance to the next one, permanently, up to 95% (so a mind is never fully immune). The resistance is rolled after the cast succeeds.

**Open:** The 95% ceiling was the agent's choice; with +30% per Charm a hero sits at 30 / 60 / 90 / 95%.
