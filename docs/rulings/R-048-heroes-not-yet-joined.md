# R-048: A hero being hired is unknown until he joins

- **Decided:** 2026-09-24, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.24-1319 §140 (item 8), v2026.09.24-1424 §143 (item 1), v2026.09.24-1453
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A wandering hero's skill levels are not shown until he has joined (only his fame is). On the order card for a hero being hired, every order is offered without level-based odds, and the mastery orders (levels 7 and 9) are not offered at all. From the turn after he joins he is an ordinary character.

**Open:** A charmed hero's card still offers all the mastery orders. Say if it should withdraw them too.
