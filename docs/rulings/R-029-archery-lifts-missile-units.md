# R-029: Archery adds 5% a level to the archers you lead

- **Decided:** 2026-09-23, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.23-1752 §19 ("Rules rulings from Toby")
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A commander's Archery adds 5% per level to the strength of the missile units he leads, in every battle.
