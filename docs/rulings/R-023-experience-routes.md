# R-023: How the slow skills are learned in the field

- **Decided:** 2026-09-21, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.21-1540 §2 (Toby's list)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Besides Practice: Sage rises from a failed Research roll and from using a magic item; Thief from carrying off a lair's gold; Explorer from a character's first step into a province their realm has not fully mapped; Naval from any sea voyage (a fleet commander at the full rate, a passenger at the small rate); Spy from spotting a hidden enemy in your own land; Druid from any battle with Creatures or Monsters on either side (the loser at half rate) and from recruiting them. Every route obeys the usual limits ([R-056](R-056-skills-rise-from-experience.md)).

**Open:** Explorer now out-trains March in bot games. Halve it, or count only provinces under 50% mapped, if it feels too fast in play.
