# R-054: A melee blow's damage

- **Decided:** 2026-09-26, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.26-1536 §236 item 4
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A melee blow deals 1–3, plus half the striker's Melee (rounded up), plus his temper's bonus, at least 1. Out-skilling a foe shows only in the chance to hit, not in damage. Arrows deal 2–5 plus half the archer's Archery.
