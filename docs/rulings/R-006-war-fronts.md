# R-006: War fronts: hosts already face to face fight at the end of Spring

- **Decided:** 2026-09-19, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG current-state summary ("WAR FRONTS (v2026.09.19-2145, Toby)")
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

An armed host that starts the turn on ground owned by a realm it is at war with, or beside an armed enemy host, fights at the end of Spring (after Spring's orders), not at the start of the turn. A battle left unresolved from last turn still resumes at the top of Spring. A new war warns both realms of each exposed host; Evil and Undead declarations, which take effect at once, give no warning.
