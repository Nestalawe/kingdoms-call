# R-143: The Crown of Command's text says it helps your own realm's characters

- **Decided:** 2026-10-31, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Question 14: the Crown of Command's text says it helps friend or foe, and the turn helps the wearer's realm only: which is right? Answer: option 1, the text follows the turn. The re-stamp of a running game's copies: decided in the rulings tranche's scoping, 15 November (KD-029's "P1-23u, Decision 3"; the row's "running games' copies of them re-stamped"). Question 175 (asked in #863 and #864): does KD-029's Decision 3 cover this row's re-stamp? Answer: yes, every copy in play is re-stamped; the maintainers' answer of 28 November.
- **Enforced by:** [`the-crown-of-commands-texts-say-it-helps-your-realms-characters`](../../tests/unit/the-crown-of-commands-texts-say-it-helps-your-realms-characters.test.ts)

The Crown of Command gives +1 to every skill of its wearer's own realm's characters in his province, as the
turn does ([R-120](R-120-the-crown-of-command-counts-everywhere.md)). Rivals beside the wearer gain nothing. Both its
texts (the desc and the lore) say so, and a copy already in a running game takes them at the turn's start.

The question was: the text said friend or foe; the turn helps the wearer's realm only. Option 1: "the text follows the
turn".

In the maintainer's words: "(1)"
