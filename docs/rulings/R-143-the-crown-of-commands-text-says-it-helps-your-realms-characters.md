- **Status:** Decided, not built

The Crown of Command gives +1 to every skill of its wearer's own realm's characters in his province, as the
turn does ([R-120](R-120-the-crown-of-command-counts-everywhere.md)); its text says so. Rivals beside the wearer gain
nothing. Today its text says "friend or foe".
