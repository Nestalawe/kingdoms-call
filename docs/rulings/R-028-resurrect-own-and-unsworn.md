# R-028: Resurrect raises only your own fallen and heroes never sworn to another crown

- **Decided:** 2026-09-23, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.23-1616 §6 (item 2); v2026.09.27-1802 §271
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Resurrect refuses a hero who ever served another realm. A realm may raise its own fallen heroes and heroes who never swore to a rival crown. The Fallen Heroes card and the map say which realm held each body.
