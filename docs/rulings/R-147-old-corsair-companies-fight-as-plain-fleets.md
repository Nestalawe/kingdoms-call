# R-147: Old Corsair companies fight as plain fleets

- **Decided:** 2026-10-31, a maintainer
- **Status:** Decided, not built
- **Source:** Question 19: two abilities of the old Corsair companies are in no book: do they keep them? Answer: option 2, the old companies lose them and fight as plain fleets. Question 173 (asked in #862): does an old Corsair company keep its own row, or take the Galleon Fleet's? Answer: it keeps its own row; the maintainers' answer of 28 November. Question 174 (asked in #862): does the Admiralty discovery's upkeep discount still apply to old Corsairs? Answer: yes, they keep it; the maintainers' answer of 28 November.
- **Enforced by:** [`an-old-corsair-company-fights-as-a-plain-fleet-and-holds-no-sea-lanes`](../../tests/unit/an-old-corsair-company-fights-as-a-plain-fleet-and-holds-no-sea-lanes.test.ts)

A Corsair company raised before the Corsair Fleet was retired ([R-109](R-109-corsairs-retired.md)) loses its
two old abilities, Cut the Line and Keep the Sea Lanes, and fights as a plain fleet. Between heroes, a winning host
at least half Corsairs no longer closes the sea to the beaten; against a province's neutral guards, a host at least
half Corsairs no longer keeps its way back to the ships when beaten.

The company keeps its own row: strength 4, speed 4, upkeep 3, not the Galleon Fleet's. It keeps the Admiralty
discovery's upkeep discount too, and the discovery's words say so. Lord Aldric's four old Corsair companies sail at
speed 4 and cost 2 gold each a turn under Admiralty.

**Note:** Decided, not yet built: the Admiralty discovery's words. Today they name only the Galleon Fleets, though
the turn gives the old Corsairs the discount too.

The question was: two abilities of the old Corsair companies were in no book. Option 2: "the old companies lose them
and fight as plain fleets".

In the maintainer's words: (2) "remove those two old Corsair abilities"
