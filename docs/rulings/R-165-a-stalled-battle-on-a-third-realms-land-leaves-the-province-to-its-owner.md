# R-165: A stalled battle on a third realm's land leaves the province to its owner

- **Decided:** 2026-11-15, a maintainer
- **Status:** Decided, not built
- **Source:** Question 56: who holds a province of a third realm when a battle there stalls, where two of the three places that say it give it to the owner? Answer: option 1, the owner keeps his province.
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

When two realms at war fight a stalled battle on a province of a third realm (an ally of one of them), the owner
keeps the province, whoever wins. That is what a lone survivor there already does, and what the war fronts' note
says. Example: Varn's and Lady Wren's hosts fight at the end of Spring on Highmere, a province
of Queen Isolde, Lady Wren's ally; Varn wins, and Highmere stays Queen Isolde's. Today the winner takes it.
