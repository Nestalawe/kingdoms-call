# R-055: Bot opponents: one strength, Normal or Aggressive

- **Decided:** 2026-09-26, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.26-1218 §197 (item 9), v2026.09.26-1536 §236 item 9
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Bots play at one strength (Easy and Normal are retired). The GM chooses, and may change mid-game, how bots treat human realms: Normal (by alignment, like any realm) or Aggressive (they declare war on humans and never offer peace). Bot-to-bot diplomacy is the same either way.
