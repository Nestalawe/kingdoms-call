# R-156: The Broker reaches what the realm's map shows

- **Decided:** 2026-11-05, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Plan question 35 (a maintainer's answer of 2026-11-05); not yet built
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A Neutral realm's Broker follows one rule of sight, the map's, as the books say: he hires a wandering hero the
realm can see now, and buys word from any province its map names. Example: Queen Isolde's realm passed through
Wendmeadow in turn 3. In turn 9 a wandering ranger stands there, with none of her heroes near: her Broker can't hire
him. Ashfall lies on her border, never entered: her Broker can buy word from it. The bots follow the same rule. Today
the turn hires a wanderer anywhere the realm has been, and buys word only from provinces it has held, visited or
explored.
