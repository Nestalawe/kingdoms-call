# R-019: A commander slain in his own battle earns nothing from it

- **Decided:** 2026-09-21, Agent's judgement
- **Status:** To confirm
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.21-1121 §1
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

A commander killed in the pre-battle duel of a battle his own order started gets no personal merit and no Imperial Title credit from it. His leaderless host still fights, still claims the province if it wins, and the realm still scores.
