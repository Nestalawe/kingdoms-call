# R-051: What each lair grade holds

- **Decided:** 2026-09-24, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.24-1739 §165–169 (Toby's four rules and rulings)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Weak lairs hold nothing that helps a hero fight or clear lairs: only kit for leading armies, spying, exploring and running the purse. Moderate lairs hold the first rung of weapons, armour, spell buffs, healing and combat items; Powerful lairs the second rung; Legendary lairs the 22 game-changing relics. A utility item in a weapon, armour or shield slot may still sit in a Weak lair (giving up the slot is the trade). A +1 in Elemental, White or Illusory counts as combat kit (Moderate); a +1 in Psychic or Necromancy is utility. A hoard is never empty: a Weak lair takes combat kit only when the treasury has nothing else left.
