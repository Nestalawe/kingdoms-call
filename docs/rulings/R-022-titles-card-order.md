# R-022: The Titles card lists your titles first

- **Decided:** 2026-09-21, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.21-1033 §3 (item 3)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Titles & Victory shows the titles this realm holds first (in the usual order), then the rest, the one nearest to won first (vacant titles at the head).

In Toby's words: “the titles the player holds first, and then the rest in order of which ones they are closest to achieving”
