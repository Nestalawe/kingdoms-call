# R-067: Ships left ashore contest the province

- **Decided:** 2026-09-27, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.27-2258 §308
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

When a beaten defender's ships are left in a coastal province (no friendly water to reach), they still hold the ground: the attacker does not take the province while they are there, and the fight resumes next phase.

In Toby's words: “ships left ashore genuinely contest the province”
