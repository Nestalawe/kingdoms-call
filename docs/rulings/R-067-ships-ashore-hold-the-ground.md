# R-067: Ships left ashore contest the province

- **Decided:** 2026-09-27, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.27-2258 §308. Question 163 (2026-11-27): do ships left ashore keep a battle's cuts when the battle resumes the same turn? Answer: option 1, the same battle: the ships keep their cuts; a maintainer's answer, 27 November.
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

When a beaten defender's ships are left in a coastal province (no friendly water to reach), they still hold the ground: the attacker does not take the province while they are there, and the fight resumes next phase.

A resumption the same turn is the same battle: the ships keep the battle's cuts until the new turn.

In the maintainer's words: “ships left ashore genuinely contest the province”
