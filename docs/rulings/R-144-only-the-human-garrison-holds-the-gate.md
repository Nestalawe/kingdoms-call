# R-144: Only the Human Garrison holds the gate

- **Decided:** 2026-10-31, a maintainer
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Question 16: the books give Hold the Gate to the Human Garrison, and the turn to every garrison: which is right? Answer: option 1, only the Human Garrison holds the gate, as the books say.
- **Enforced by:** [`the-human-units-abilities-on-the-battle-grid-and-at-their-fate-after-a-defeat`](../../tests/unit/the-human-units-abilities-on-the-battle-grid-and-at-their-fate-after-a-defeat.test.ts)

Hold the Gate (never hit from the side when defending) belongs to the Human Garrison alone, as both rulebooks
say. Elven, Dwarf and Orc Garrisons have their own two abilities; defending at the wrapped end of a line, they take
the side hit (×0.9). Example: Lady Wren's Orc Garrison defends Bonestone, and Varn's wider line wraps round its end:
the Orc Garrison is hit from the side.

The question was: the books give Hold the Gate to the Human Garrison, the turn to every garrison. Option 1: "only the
Human Garrison holds the gate, as the books say".

In the maintainer's words: "(1)"
