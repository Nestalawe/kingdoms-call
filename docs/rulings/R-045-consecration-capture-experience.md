# R-045: Taking holy ground: experience by the conqueror's crown

- **Decided:** 2026-09-24, Toby
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** DEV_LOG v2026.09.24-1319 §139 (item 7)
- **Enforced by:** no test yet

Capturing a consecrated province breaks its consecration at once. The capturing commander's own units may each rise a quality tier: Undead 100% chance, Evil 50%, every other crown none ("the troops take no pride in the sacrilege").
